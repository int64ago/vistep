## Same geometry, same phase

In a planar section, the upper scattering point is O and the lower point B, separated by d. A is B projected back to the incident wavefront through O; C is B projected forward to the outgoing wavefront through O. Actual coordinates give AB=BC=d sinθ. Dashed AO and OC are perpendicular to the corresponding ray directions. The extra path is AB+BC, never a decorative vertical segment.

The two lower traces use the resulting phase 2π(AB+BC)/λ on one shared clock, and their sum uses the same sampled values. The electronic angular signal independently sums eight plane amplitudes.
