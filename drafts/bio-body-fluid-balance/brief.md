## Phenomenon and misconception

How can cells remain within a stable range while conditions change? Account for tracer exchange, edit household temperature records, and compare feedback balances for heat, water and sodium. Separate measured or reported observations from constructed mechanism trajectories. No historical numerical trace is fabricated.

## Shots

- plasma-tissue: Tracer can exchange while red cells stay in blood.
- lymph-return: Part of the interstitial route returns through lymph.
- temperature-record: Observations appear individually, not from a theoretical curve.
- temperature-compare: Compare people on one scale and air on a separate scale.
- heat-balance: Heat-loss responses reduce the deviation while preserving the balance.
- water-adh: ADH changes water retention; it does not create water.
- sodium-aldosterone: Sodium retention and water retention are related but distinct.
