Misconception: a nearby charged rod creates charge, or neutral means that a conductor contains no charges. Follow the same negative carriers while the pair's total charge stays zero.

Benchmark: equal isolated conductors without an external source divide a total charge equally; the induced pair always sums to zero. Counterexample: withdrawing the rod before separation restores neutrality, whereas separating first retains opposite net charges. Electroscope neutral induction leaves net charge zero even while the foil opens.
