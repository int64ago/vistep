## Model and scientific limits

The historical diagrams and explanatory animation use separate state producers. Historical spots have independent sample identities and immutable localizations. Their number and intensity are illustrative, not measured proportions. The 3 / 17 / 117 min labels reproduce textbook observations and never serve as an animation clock.

The explanatory route retains the prior continuous three-dimensional ER/Golgi lumen curves, carrier trajectories, plasma-membrane intersections and projection geometry. A normalized teaching clock reparameterizes those curves. Twenty-four cargo identities remain present in all transport scenarios; the count is an explanatory inventory, not a historical measurement. The comparison display bins those identities by compartment and is explicitly a location ledger, not a spatial trajectory.

The ER exit gate affects only cargo arriving while the exit is blocked. Cargo already downstream is not rewound or frozen. Restoration continues the held cohort from its stopped position. This is a conditional intervention: the model assumes other processes can proceed. It does not claim a specific drug affects only one transport step, nor that stopping all ATP production would leave downstream traffic unchanged. Early and late blocks are separate comparison scenarios.

A magnified planar budding/fusion section derives membrane endpoints and its outer carrier arc from one circle–plane intersection. Cargo stays in the connected lumen/carrier region. The plasma-membrane close-up retains the prior circle–ellipse intersection and relaxation, including membrane retention. Both views explain topology and continuity; they do not simulate molecular fusion proteins, lipid area accounting or all intra-Golgi transport mechanisms. A cargo ring is an identity marker, not the letter P or a molecular scale label.
