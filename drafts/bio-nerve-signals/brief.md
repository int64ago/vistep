## Independent local membrane model

Use the modern convention `V = inside − outside`. The scene integrates two conductance currents using a closed-form constant-conductance step and the same exact mean voltage for both charge integrals. Units are mV, ms, mS/cm², µF/cm² and nC/cm². Na/K reversal potentials are fixed at +60/−90 mV for this short teaching interval. Basal conductances balance at −70 mV. Conductance opening times are prescribed; this is not the full Hodgkin–Huxley voltage-gating system and does not solve threshold, refractory behavior, propagation or changing ion reservoirs.

The invariant is `C ΔV = QNa,in − QK,out`; opposite membrane surfaces have equal and opposite charge. The voltage trace is reconstructed from that account, not replayed as a fixed waveform. Two selected ion markers are illustrative identities, not the entire ionic population or measured microscopic trajectories. Unshown basal currents remain in the charge account. No pump forcibly resets the waveform.

An independent spherical-cell estimate uses radius 10 µm, capacitance density 1 µF/cm², a 100 mV change and one 150 mM monovalent reservoir. The required net capacitive charge is about 1.26 pC, or 0.0021% of that reservoir. This is net charge imbalance, not the sum of opposing ionic fluxes. The bulk remains approximately electrically neutral.
