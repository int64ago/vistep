The scene begins with a discrepancy: both rear screens are lit, but only one sample reveals a clear side beam. Its central claim is that light passing through a place and light reaching an observer from that place are different conditions. A connected source–particle–eye route, a selective blocker, and a driven-charge close-up make the missing cause visible. Dilution and equal-power color comparison then test predictions; a dusty sunbeam transfers the explanation while withholding an invalid numerical extrapolation.
