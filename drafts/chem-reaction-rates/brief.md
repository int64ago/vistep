## Model and visual contract

- Macroscopic peroxide/thiosulfate constants remain stated illustrative parameters. They are not a fit to book measurements. NaN and nonfinite time inputs now produce finite fallback states.
- Thermal E1–E8 are deliberately selected near-threshold cases. Energy is scaled with Kelvin temperature; the threshold stays fixed. Counts are not a representative thermal population and never fit the macroscopic rate constant. E6 retains its ring through motion.
- Concentration close-ups use 6 and 12 representative events over the same window, with the identical eligibility rule. This is not a universal empirical reaction order. Event IDs persist, arrivals are counted only when their own transfer ends, and the last event arrives before the endpoint.
- The optical threshold uses the same sulfur/transmission model. At 0.2 mL of 0.1 M stock in 4 mL, maximum sulfur 0.005 M is below ln(2)/40≈ 0.0173 M. The endpoint is unreachable despite nonzero reaction.
- Catalytic curves share their initial/final energies and show which path is catalyzed. No ranking is inferred between unequal real catalyst conditions.
- HI spheres reconstruct the textbook's two-step illustration, not measured trajectories or a complete dynamical energy balance with surroundings.
- Each phone shot uses a 255 px high coordinate space at its actual width, 16 px labels, short causal captions and 44 px controls. Desktop uses its own bounded width. Important objects are not hidden in a narrow-screen alternative.
