# Particle evidence: visible products, conserved quantities, independent checks

## Scope and misconception

A candidate event is not a discovery, and laboratory energy alone is not invariant mass. Start from two calorimeter deposits, charged-track curvature, or a beam-energy scan; make conservation determine a physical inference before testing it against fixed observations. Preserve twelve chapter IDs and seven textbook windows in their original order. Only the six Higgs, antihyperon and J/ψ cues change; the six gamma-absorption, capture and parity spoken cues remain byte-identical. Their apparatus geometry is retained, with narrow-screen text sizing and shorter equivalent labels.

## Directed causal views

1. **Higgs event:** two photon deposits without charged inner tracks → add energy and vector momentum → boost the same 125 GeV/c² parent. Desktop retains detector and momentum ledger together; phone moves from the detector to a full-size ledger. Momentum arrows use one energy-proportional scale because photon momentum is E/c.
2. **Higgs evidence:** fixed ATLAS figure readings → published joint-fit background component and residual → a withheld CMS comparison → separately reported mass. This does not produce a new significance calculation or a background-only refit.
3. **Antihyperon A:** positive parent curvature and positive pion → charge and missing momentum → B and the trackless AB gap. The measured parent-curvature momentum is kept distinct from the later conditional reconstruction that uses AB as input.
4. **Antihyperon B:** measured AB constrains a roughly 1.18 GeV/c² parent → ordinary-neutron kinetic budget is too small for the measured charged-product minimum → annihilation supplies additional rest energy. The diagram omits target residue and unseen neutral products and is not a closed six-track nuclear-reaction account.
5. **J/ψ fixed target:** field and curvature give momenta → lower field changes hit positions on the same fixed detector plane at x = 8 m → predict a stable reconstructed mass → reveal the historical altered-field sample. Phone uses a local enlargement of the actual hit displacement, not a moving detector.
6. **J/ψ collider:** equal head-on beam momenta cancel → center-of-mass energy is 2E → scan fixed published cross-section points → compare the scale with fixed-target mass. Preserve the figure's broken horizontal axis, logarithmic nb axis, historical calibration and resolution limits.
