# Unit cells and diffraction

Four textbook positions, eight chapters, 241 s. Elective 2 P73–76 and elective 3 P19–21 text read; P74, P75 and organic P20 actually rendered/viewed. The separate molecular/network target is assigned to chem-crystal-bonding. Conventional vs primitive cell terminology, aperiodic vs amorphous order, finite planar transforms vs actual 3D diffraction, powder averaging, phase limitations and modern vs historical data are explicit.

Independent models: boundary-derived 1/8,1/4,1/2,1 counts; Cu FCC and NaCl sites; area-preserving Robinson subdivision with fivefold spatial symmetry; coherent Fourier intensity; fixed-seed minimum-separated disorder; 3D Debye powder intensity using real pair distances; a genuine one-parameter gradient/backtracking refinement against immutable synthetic intensities; and published artemisinin coordinates/connections. The artemisinin source is Fetah et al. 2026 Form I_1 (296 K, 0.3 GPa), not historical discovery data. Direct COD 1563559 metadata was accessible but its CIF retrieval failed, so no unverified coordinates were substituted. The 2026 article's public atomic and bond tables were read directly; O—O, carbonyl length and peroxide angle independently agree with that table.

Expensive grid and Debye calculations run in a scene-specific Worker, with cleanup, loading and heatmap retry. No shared renderer or registry changes. Structured tests check cell contributions, substitution area/symmetry, Bragg benchmark/Friedel/translation invariants, random separation and rotational powder invariance, actual monotonic fitting, and published bond-angle benchmarks. Model-derived heatmap stills and actual component SVG geometry self-review are separate from browser playback. Root must check all chapters, worker-failure behavior, 320/390/desktop complete player, real-space/reciprocal correspondence, labels and projected geometry, model-derived cover, seeking/watch isolation, and both voices. Physical-device performance is not measured.
