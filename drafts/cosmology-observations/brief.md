## What should change in the reader's understanding?

The color of a galaxy is not a redshift measurement; a recognized line pattern gives a wavelength ratio. A line drawn through points generated by its own equation is not independent evidence. Fixed historical distance and velocity records constrain the explanation, while a separate uniform expansion model explains why farther separations grow more in the same time. A lone microwave excess is not already a precise blackbody temperature: receiver calibration, the original account and later spectral evidence have distinct roles. Finally, expansion changes photon wavelength and energy and preserves a cooling blackbody shape.
