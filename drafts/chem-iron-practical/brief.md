## Model and representation

`practicalNet` evaluates balanced declared net reactions with explicit species, atom totals, charges and limiting extents. It includes oxygen, hydrogen and ligand elements, not merely iron/Mn counters. `practicalFrame` provides integer event recipes, persistent atom and electron IDs, positions, formal states and a ledger including credits in transit. Measured watch choreography may finish the donor and receiver spatial paths at different spoken moments, but charge/arrival gates still control those motions and share the same ledger. In the first chapter Fe reaches water while Cu²⁺ still awaits the two in-transit credits. Two oxidized iodine identities may form I₂ before the Fe receivers finish accepting their credits; the remaining iodine stays I⁻. Metal products land on the selected metal surface; they are not created as separate ghost atoms.

The Mn acceptor is the complete net packet MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 4H₂O. It is never drawn as a bare Mn⁷⁺ ion or a succession of invented stable MnO₄ charges. `practicalAcidAtoms` follows one selected net allocation with 13 persistent Mn/O/H IDs. These paths do not claim elementary mechanisms, isotope provenance or kinetic rates. Other acidity branches are outside the model.

`practicalBinding` shows one selected ligand/water exchange with the central Fe oxidation state unchanged. This is not a full equilibrium distribution. FeII coordination is omitted, not prohibited. The iodine host grouping is qualitative and does not claim a unique I₃⁻ structure. Colour is not calibrated absorbance.

Each experiment is a fresh sample. The route map records textbook reactions and has no energy axis. Controls cannot rewrite earlier textbook records. Exact teaching counts are not reagent recipes; conservation constrains a declared reaction but does not prove arbitrary feasibility.
