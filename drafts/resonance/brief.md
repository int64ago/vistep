# Resonance direction

Track one mass and the applied force. Initial intuition: a stronger response needs a stronger push. Turning point: the same force builds a larger displacement near the natural frequency. Result: damping limits accumulation. Sources: OpenStax Forced Oscillations, checked 2026-10-17. Original vector artwork, no copied images. Horizontal spring model is intentionally a planar instrument, not ornamental 3D.

Phone: 360-unit dedicated field, one oscillator above the power strip and wide response curve; desktop uses 720 units. No second side panel.
