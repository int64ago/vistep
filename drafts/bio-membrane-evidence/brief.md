## Misconceptions and tracked objects

A pre-drawn bilayer cannot serve as evidence for a bilayer. Dark image bands are not chemical names; low tension does not uniquely locate proteins; mobility does not imply that every protein is unrestricted. Prerequisites are particles, water/lipid phases, membrane sidedness and simple ratios.

The decisive area shot begins with unresolved red cells, then displays independently measured A and B as equal-width rectangles. Original human-cell values are 0.47 and 0.92 m². Only afterward do one-layer and two-layer hypotheses predict 0.47 and 0.94 m². Forty stable lipid IDs then explain ideal footprints, with the brown head retained through folding. The loss prediction is a separate hypothetical comparison; original observations remain immutable.

Fusion observations are two distinct time-sampled representative reconstructions at 5 and 40 min. They never share continuous molecular IDs. Later, a clearly labeled one-dimensional closed-path model tracks random motion. Its angular D has units rad² per teaching-time unit; display radius is not a physical diffusion parameter.
