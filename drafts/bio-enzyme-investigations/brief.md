## Model and limits

- The energy coordinate is an illustrative Boltzmann state model in units of R × Tref. It is not a Maxwell speed distribution or a measured activation barrier. The enlarged tail and its relative density/energy axes are explicit. Kinetic crossing probabilities are obtained from the same density.
- Catalysis changes rate, not the theoretical stoichiometric inventory. Two peroxide units can produce one oxygen unit. Numerical rate constants, pre-exposure temperatures and durations are chosen for explanation and are not a fitted catalase or amylase experiment.
- The generic fold survival law integrates temperature and exposure time. The same hazard drives the representative peptide shape and active population. It preserves the continuous peptide trace rather than depicting thermal injury as peptide-bond cleavage. The model selects persistent damage; it does not assert that all denaturation in all proteins is irreversible.
- pH observations use the same bubble display scale for both samples and instantaneous model flux. The generic group model has illustrative pKa values and does not identify actual liver-catalase residues. Exploring a new pH recomputes a comparison scenario; it is not a claim that an already damaged sample instantly refolds.
- The glucan drawing omits most side groups. It preserves the displayed reactive atoms and net water balance while showing one α(1→4) cleavage. It is neither an atomistic catalytic mechanism nor a claim that amylase converts all starch directly to glucose. Contact endpoints are derived from the actual transformed substrate atoms and vanish as those atoms leave the enzyme.
- Historical diagrams are explanatory reconstructions. The stomach cage excludes direct grinding as the only explanation; the cell-free experiment does not purify a single enzyme; urease composition and catalytic activity require separate evidence. Urease products are aqueous material-equivalent signals, not an invented gas observation.
