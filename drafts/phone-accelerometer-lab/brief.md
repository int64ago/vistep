# Phone accelerometer: same motion, different reported quantity

Resolve the misleading expectation that any phone app reads−g in free fall. Preserve textbook learning objective while explicitly separating specific force from gravity-corrected coordinate acceleration. Track one flat phone with upward screen-normal z, plus both traces from the identical motion model. Six chapters: supported rest, freefall, catch/output correction, lift stages, compare raw/corrected lift, spring-force-meter alternative. First three belong to required1§2.4, last three to§4.6.

The phone changes position continuously at release and catch. Specific force jumps only at idealized support transitions. Hand contact is visible when present; no hand follows the freely falling phone. Desktop puts device beside readings; phone viewport puts both readings in two columns below the device, followed by one paired trace. Text remains CSS16px or larger. Track and pause state reconstruct from Showcase. Source: phyphox official acceleration-with-g documentation. Independent tests check the zero raw freefall value, piecewise trajectory continuity, catch impulse and lift displacement.
