9 chapters, 234 s. Three actual apparatuses: boiling bath → insulated mixing cup with thermometer; sealed syringe connected by tubing to absolute gauge and p–V or reciprocal graph; stoppered flask with horizontal movable water plug. Variants use contiguous triples. Misconceptions: arithmetic mean temperature, gauge pressure in Boyle law, ignoring dead volume, rapid compression treated as isothermal, Celsius ratios. Constant two-lump thermal conductance conserves energy; analytical ideal-gas constraints produce all readouts and motion.
