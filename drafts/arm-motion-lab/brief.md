## B23 principle and presentation review

The misconception is that fast fingertip motion belongs only to the last joint, or that a released drop needs a new outward force. Prior knowledge is velocity as a vector and acceleration as its change. One fingertip and its prescribed drop are tracked; no adhesion threshold, athlete reconstruction or distance prediction is claimed.
