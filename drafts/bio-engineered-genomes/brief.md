## Evidence and limits

[Gibson et al. (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1190719), original characterization text and Figs. 4–5, supports the synthetic-genome identity and colony phenotype. The scene redraws schematic observations rather than claiming laboratory footage or recreating gel intensities. It does not claim four marker checks alone establish every base. The explanatory machines are post-transplant context; there is no invented passage of a whole chromosome through an intact membrane.

[Shao et al. (2018)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0382-x), printed P334, Fig. 5c left panel, supplies 20 independently extracted mean coordinates for haploid BY4742 and SY14. The frozen source JSON retains DOI, PDF and vector hashes, exact plotted positions and marker identifiers. These are means from three biological replicates, not raw replicate values. Original SEM bars are omitted with an explicit label. Approximate figure-reading bounds are ±0.06 h and ±0.03 OD, not experimental uncertainty. OD₆₀₀ is not a direct viable-cell count. Connecting lines guide the eye. Revelation is a teaching edit, not a claim of an original held-out prediction test.

The 2018 study reorganized chromosomes within existing yeast cells, with centromere, telomere and other deletions. It is distinct from 2010 bacterial genome transplantation. The textbook synopsis compresses those stages; the article preserves its learning context while following the primary studies' mechanisms.

The [reporter chemistry source](https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/technical-documents/technical-article/genomics/cloning-and-expression/blue-white-screening) supports enzymatic X-gal cleavage followed by subsequent blue-product formation. The renderer retains two precursor identities and shows pigment only after their release. Later oxidation, dimerization and intermediate steps are combined; this is neither a full atom ledger nor one-substrate-to-one-pigment stoichiometry. Enzyme shape and color do not become the pigment.

RNA nodes, peptide beads and chromosome blocks are representative objects, not actual sequences, counts or a molecular structure solution. Event time is teaching order, not measured kinetics. Expression controls do not predict whole-cell viability. Existing RNA can support translation without transcription occurring now; the controls therefore avoid a single simultaneous all-inputs gate.
