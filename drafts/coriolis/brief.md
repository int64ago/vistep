# Coriolis: production direction

Initial intuition: a curved trail must mean a new horizontal force. Track one gold puck and match its equal-time dots across cameras. Turning point: rotate its coordinates back and recover exactly the same straight worldline.

Opening sketch: two circular coordinate plates, one puck and a straight dotted trajectory. Middle: the fixed camera retains a straight trail while the table camera traces a curve. Result: red and blue acceleration terms add to the second derivative in rotating coordinates; fading rotation restores coincidence.

Desktop uses paired same-scale plates. Phone uses the current camera with a persistent fixed-trajectory witness strip; no directional speech cues depend on the layout. All labels outside the SVG stay at 16 CSS pixels. Exploration changes actual release conditions; elapsed time is reconstructed analytically.

MIT's rotating-frame teaching video and relative-motion lecture were opened on 2026-10-17. Equations are independently implemented as an exact coordinate transform. The flat frictionless table omits sphere, wind, pressure, drag and drain dynamics. No 3D claim is made: this is a plan-view coordinate instrument.
