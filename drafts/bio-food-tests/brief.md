# Food-assay production brief

Resolve the misconception that every color change detects the same substance. Track the same sample and a reagent-matched water blank. Distinct visual forms: two tubes in a visible warm water bath; a thin-section field with retained lipid droplets and removable background; two tubes showing the Biuret endpoint.

Primary/official basis: NIE practical handbook Practical 1; curricular details independently read in local Required 1 P18–19. Qualitative state machine, not quantitative kinetics. The Fehling water bath is 50–65 °C in this textbook; the demonstration does not transplant Benedict reagent instructions.

Benchmarks and counterexamples: glucose-containing pear sample versus water and unhydrolyzed sucrose; positive protein without warming; invalid addition order flagged; Sudan background washing retains lipid stain. Tests cover these independently. Droplet identity is retained before and after washing.

Desktop: tall tube pair with blank. Phone: the same pair remains side by side with HTML labels; the lipid view occupies its own field. Each stage changes actual assay state. No animation timers. Explore values never influence directed watch states.
