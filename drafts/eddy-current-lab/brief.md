## Scientific model and evidence identity

This is a calculated mechanism demonstration, not a re-created experimental dataset. The original valid-domain meter, spring, frame, disc and pipe motion outputs are preserved within numerical roundoff. New reciprocal coupling computes ε = −Kq̇, I = ε/R and generalized force K I, so mechanical power removed equals I²R.

- Meter: moving-coil effective radial-field coupling, open/closed comparison and same initial mechanical energy.
- Frame: one actual vertical frame, B direction and frame normal in one oblique 3D projection, Φ = K cos(θmagnet − θframe). Instantaneous current is distinct from cycle-averaged motion and power. Equal-speed counterfactual shows zero induced torque at that instant; it is not a claim of sustained synchronous motion against friction.
- Disc: two local equivalent loops straddle opposite edges of one field patch. Their opposite currents sum to the original braking coefficient. Changing B sign reverses I but not braking torque. No field markers remain at B = 0. The loop model is explicitly not a solved eddy-current field mesh.
- Tube: physically fixed ring samples use point-dipole flux, actual ring resistance and the same reciprocal force law. The analytic long-pipe integral matches c = 0.35 kg/s. The displayed A ring is the same ring as its readout. A slit interrupts full loops; c = 0.012 represents residual local loss separately, with qualitative local-loop geometry, not invented precise current values. A held magnet has visible external support, nonzero flux, zero emf/current; post-fall catch energy and support are separate from Joule heat.
- Spring: linearized flux gradient, magnet attachment and coil switch geometry; open/closed energy split.

Finite invalid values and unphysical denominators reject before producing SVG NaN. Time is nonnegative. Self-inductance, finite-pipe end corrections, finite-magnet fields, skin effects and saturation are outside scope. The enlarged magnet icon is not a geometric input.
