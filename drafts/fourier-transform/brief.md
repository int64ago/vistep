# Fourier decomposition

Track the same 64 sample identities across synthesis, direct DFT and inverse reconstruction. Opening: complex waveform. Turn: sine-product projection cancels at k=2 but accumulates at k=3. Result: changing phase preserves magnitude; a fractional-cycle component leaks under a finite rectangular window.

Desktop uses a harmonic score and a sum; phone preserves the current score or one instrument, measured in actual CSS-pixel SVG units. No audio oscillator is implied. Eight distinct causal chapters, planned 160s. DFT is computed directly; FFT is only a fast algorithm, not the phenomenon. The rectangular-window limitation is visible and disclosed.
