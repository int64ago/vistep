# Code of Conduct

vistep welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, abilities, languages and levels of experience. Treat one another with respect. Critique ideas and work with specific, useful observations; give people room to learn and disagree.

We expect considerate language, constructive feedback, respect for privacy and appropriate credit for others' work. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where someone represents this project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, limit participation or ban accounts when needed. Responses should reflect the severity and pattern of the behavior, and affected people may ask for reconsideration.

Report concerns privately to **int64ago@gmail.com**, the maintainer's public contact address. Include links and enough context to assess the situation; do not publish personal information in an issue. Reports will be handled with care and shared only as necessary to respond. There is no guaranteed response time for this volunteer-maintained project.

中文：欢迎不同背景、语言和经验的参与者。讨论请针对具体工作，尊重隐私与署名，不容许骚扰、歧视、威胁或未经许可公开他人信息。问题可通过上述维护者邮箱私下反馈；维护者会按严重程度处理，并接受复议。
