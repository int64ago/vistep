# Eddy-current brake: close the current paths before drawing the brake force

Owner `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Misconceptions: a magnetic field always brakes a moving conductor; an aluminium sheet needs ferromagnetic attraction; cutting slots only removes mass; an eddy brake can hold a stationary object; magnetic drag consumes energy without heat. Prerequisites: induction, Ohm's law and magnetic force on current. Distinct from Hall effect: conservative transverse fields can cancel a uniform motional field, but a spatially localized field drives non-conservative closed currents.

Primary sources verified2026-10-18: OpenStax University Physics2§13.5 and College Physics2e§23.4 for slotted plates and magnetic damping; MIT8.02 eddy-braking/Joule-heating presentation; MIT thin-sheet visualization for the spatial problem. This model uses a low-speed electroquasistatic thin sheet, not the full self-inductive/high-speed solution of the MIT visualization.

Uniform1mm aluminium sheet, σ=3.5×10⁷ S/m, observed320×160mm region; long-strip direction continues outside the view. Prescribed longitudinal velocity0–50mm/s. Bz=B0 exp[−(x/.04m)^4−(y/.055m)^4], with an optional truly uniform B benchmark. Grid33×24, normal insulating boundaries at remote x truncation and real y edges; longitudinal zero-width insulating cuts divide the metal into1/2/4/8 lanes. Same area and conductivity, no mass-removal explanation. Far x field tails are negligible; finite-boundary/truncation and finite-grid approximation disclosed.

The independent model solves ∇·J=0 with J=σ(−∇φ+v×B) on a conservative finite-volume resistor graph. Edge currents derive from node potential differences plus motional EMF. Charge residual at every node, boundary no-through-current, and loop streamfunction are checked. Closed streamfunction contours are extracted and stitched; no decorative circles. The current signs also set tangent-arrow direction. Insulating cuts have no crossing edges and current contours cannot bridge them.

Lorentz force F_x=∫J_y Bz dV is summed independently from Joule heat∫J²/σ dV; −F_x v=Pheat within solve tolerance. Default v30mm/s,B0.2T; sweep B0.1→0.3T. Uniform B admits electrostatic cancellation and zero eddy braking. Velocity reversal reverses both current and drag; B reversal reverses current but not drag. At rest both vanish. Low magnetic Reynolds number (about0.05 at default scale, below0.1 at speed limit) supports neglecting induced-field feedback. No skin effect, self-inductance, magnetic saturation, ferromagnetic hysteresis or bearing/friction brake.

Six chapters: uniform-field counterexample; localized-field closed loops and opposing Lorentz force; reverse prescribed velocity; approach rest and lose holding force; split8 lanes to interrupt large loops; steady-drive mechanical loss becomes heat and grows with B². Changes compare quasistatic states. Prescribed kinematic travel integrates velocity over8s of physical drift per chapter; no unmodeled freely falling mass or claim of a full acceleration energy balance.
