# Universal joint — a full turn does not imply uniform speed

Owner: `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Independent manufactured 3D: two colored forks, ring bearings with open bores, perpendicular steel trunnions, connected shafts and radial timing marks. A paired-joint view maintains the intermediate yokes in the correct shared spatial phase. Color follows each continuous shaft and both its yokes.

Misconception: a universal joint that transmits a full revolution must transmit constant instantaneous speed; any two joints cancel. Prerequisite: rotation, speed, perpendicular directions. Distinction from differential/gears: this is speed ripple caused by intersecting-axis constraints, not gear ratio or distribution of torque.

Sources checked: [SDP/SI, Universal Joints technical reference: Kinematics and Phasing](https://sdp-si.com/Catalogs/D757-Couplings-Universal-Joints3.php), manufacturer technical material, available in search retrieval (direct fetch later returned 403). Supports cross-and-yoke construction, two speed cycles per turn, increasing variation with bend, and equal-angle correctly phased double-joint cancellation while the intermediate shaft continues to vary. Geometry, equations and drawings are independently constructed. No manufacturer artwork is reproduced.

Seven shots: zero bend; establish the bend at a frozen phase; two speed cycles in one turn; compare bend at the same phase; count complete input/output turns; equal-angle correctly phased double joint; unequal-angle counterexample. The ratio curve is evaluated from the same model that poses both forks and the cross. In paired view, dashed trace is intermediate speed and solid is final output. Input 120 rpm is the physical reference; animation is deliberately slow. Configuration comparisons freeze phase, rather than implying a stopped input has 120 rpm.

Analytic model: input axis a=X, intermediate axis b=(cosβ,sinβ,0), input pin u=(0,cosθ,sinθ), intermediate pin v=normalize(b×u). This enforces each bearing pin perpendicular to its shaft and both cross pins perpendicular. Continuous angle ψ=atan2(sinθ,cosβ cosθ), with derivative cosβ/(sin²θ+cos²β cos²θ). Paired final axis c=(cos(β−γ),sin(β−γ),0), output pin normalize(v×c), angle χ=atan2(cosγ sinψ,cosψ). Exact cancellation at β=γ. Independent 30° benchmarks are √3/2 and 2/√3. Conventions shift textbook angular origins by a quarter-turn, not extrema or period.

Limits: rigid kinematics, fixed correct intermediate phasing, no torque/dynamic/stress/friction/backlash/interference prediction. UI bends 0–35° are pedagogical, not a manufacturer's limit.
