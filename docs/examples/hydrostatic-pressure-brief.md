## Question and distinct addition

Resolve more water→more bottom pressure, gauge=absolute and same pressure→same total force. Prerequisites: liquid weight, depth and normal force. Connect `buoyancy` pressure difference with `hydraulic-brake` pA. Independent form is connected shaped laboratory glassware and a diaphragm microscope, not the previous floating object.

## Art and six causal additions

Pale glasswork, a shared liquid plane, gold probes and real connecting channels. Physical elevation from the same profile drives glass sides, fill, sensor location and weights. Static comparison changes fill amount, not an implicit fixed-mass flow process.

1. Same-depth probes descend: gauge pressure grows with vertical h.
2. Common liquid level changes, bottom probes compare three shapes with same p.
3. Fixed h, vary density: all p gradients change together.
4. Fixed h/ρ, raise common surface gas p0: absolute shifts but hydrostatic excess stays.
5. Show liquid weights and vertical wall components: unequal W reconciles equal base force. Blue arrow length uses half total wall vertical force on each side; not full pressure-normal vector.
6. Separate full-width diaphragm close-up, matched face/edge diameter: area1→4cm², p fixed and F×4. Not a subpixel dot-only control.
