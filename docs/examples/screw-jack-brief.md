# Screw jack — one lead per turn, conditional self-locking

Owner: `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Medium: original Three.js square-thread cutaway, shared-model flat fallback and one-turn unwrapping. Main tracked object: 1000 N axial load. Misconception: torque reduction is free work; every screw self-locks. Prerequisites: lever/ramp and rotation. Adds physical helical contact, lead versus pitch and friction-angle boundary.

Primary source: [NPTEL Mechanics, Module III, Lesson 17, pp. 1–5](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/112105164/lec17.pdf). Claims: lead = starts × pitch, helix angle, square-thread raising/lowering torque, self-locking if friction angle exceeds helix angle. Limits: single start, square thread, quasi-static, ideal thrust bearing, no strength or lubrication prediction. Benchmark uses the source's 400 N, 10 mm diameter, 1.5 mm lead, μ = 0.2 example: approximately 0.500 N·m raising and 0.302 N·m lowering. Counterexample: μ = 0 permits back-driving.

Geometry: closed rectangular helical solids, 64 sections per revolution; nut back-half sectors have capped radial ends. Screw crest/valley radii 22/18 mm, mating nut extends inward to 18.5 mm. External thickness is 0.4 lead; internal ridge is 0.6 lead at half-lead phase, giving ideal face contact. Both share lead. Rotation about Three's Y axis is negative for increasing authored phase, paired with positive axial lead displacement. The compact bench-scale handle radius is 45 mm, and both 3D/fallback extents derive from `parameters.handle`; force analysis uses that same radius. A swivel saddle avoids rotating the load. Fixed nut support reaches the base. Every geometry replacement is released through Studio disposal; lead changes are discrete comparative configurations, not continuous renderer resets.

Six shots: lift one lead; unwrap one turn; frictionless rotational work; double lead at equal turns; introduce friction and heat; reduce friction through self-locking boundary. Final two show quasi-static required torque, not an invented acceleration after release. Phone uses a compact 280 px model and HTML readouts; the unwrapped view replaces the full assembly for its chapter. Fallback retains load, nut, helix and handle.
