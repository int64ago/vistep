# Lunar eclipse — author brief

Misconceptions: every full Moon is eclipsed; lunar center inside shadow is enough for totality; umbral area equals brightness loss; total eclipse means absolutely no light or one fixed red. Different from solar eclipse: Earth casts the shadow on the Moon, with node-dependent offset and a magnified lunar disk rather than terrestrial observer coverage.
