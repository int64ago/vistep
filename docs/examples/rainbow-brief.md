## Model and limits

Snell and reflection laws determine actual sphere contacts. IAPWS R9-97 coefficients provide the water index at 293.15 K and 998.2 kg/m³, with air approximated as n=1. Fresnel s/p powers remain separate until summation. Equal-area incident sampling produces finite angular power bins, not sky radiance. Exact outgoing rays meet or miss the same eye. The sky uses a gnomonic cone and geometric horizon. Wave effects, finite solar disk, nonspherical drops and multiple scattering are excluded.
