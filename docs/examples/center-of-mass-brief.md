# Center of mass — internal motion cannot move the whole center by itself

Owner: `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. A physical freely translating air-track platform with a guided ballast stack, drawn in a fixed laboratory frame. Moving cargo visibly shifts the platform oppositely while a weighted center marker stays fixed. Background position ticks remain fixed, and later uniform translation or external acceleration makes the center marker move across them. No hidden camera tracking.

Misconceptions: center of mass is always the geometric midpoint; moving inside can propel an isolated system; no external force means the center must be stationary. Distinction from lever: this is translation and momentum under internal motion, not a pivot's static torque balance. Distinction from elastic-collision: there is prescribed finite internal travel without an impact. Distinction from angular-momentum: mass-weighted linear motion rather than radial redistribution about a fixed axis.
