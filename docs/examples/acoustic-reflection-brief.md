## Misconceptions and addition

A reflected pressure pulse and reflected particle velocity do not share the same inversion rule. Pressure amplitude >1 is not energy amplification. Sound-speed mismatch alone does not require reflection. Prerequisite: pressure disturbance and wave direction. Adds impedance and energy conditions to `ultrasound`, and prepares `standing-wave`/organ-pipe boundary conditions without repeating standing-wave mode shape. No audible experiment is synthesized or auto-played.
