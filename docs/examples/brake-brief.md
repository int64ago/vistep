# Hydraulic brakes — production brief

[简体中文](../zh-CN/examples/brake-brief.md)

Part of the [50-scene expansion](../expansion-50.md). Start with a rotor turning between two stationary pads, then open the hand-operated master cylinder and opposing-piston caliper. A continuous hose and internal galleries connect the pressure chambers. A copper fluid tint indicates pressure; it must not suggest fluid circulating around a loop.

Use a pale ceramic workbench, graphite metal, brushed rotor, muted red caliper and restrained amber fluid. Wide views establish the hardware; the camera approaches the master cylinder and pad contact. A separate, clearly enlarged sectional drawing exposes the tiny gaps and compensation port. On a phone, move the master above the rotor and reconstruct the hose around that arrangement.

Eight chapters: first squeeze, compensation-port closure, gap take-up, equal pressure and unequal areas, larger master-cylinder tradeoff, compressible trapped air, motion becoming heat, and release. Automatic progression changes geometric positions and computed state. Manual stroke and air volume are optional; the default film carries the explanation.

The teaching assembly specifies a 10 mm master bore (variable 8–14 mm), two 18 mm caliper pistons, 0.25 mm pad gaps, a 4:1 ideal lever, and explicit fluid/hose and pad compliance. It is not a replica or a parts-selection tool. The model conserves displaced volume, uses gauge pressure, and solves isothermal trapped-air compression. A separate ideal rolling bicycle model converts brake work to heat without attributing all dissipated energy to the rider's hand. Seal friction, tire grip, load transfer, fade and thermal diffusion are excluded.

Sources: [OpenStax hydraulics](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-3-pascals-principle-and-hydraulics), [Shimano's brake explanation](https://bike.shimano.com/en-SG/stories/article/the-easy-to-understand-guide-to-brakes.html), [Brembo on the compensation-port/free-stroke mechanism](https://www.brembo.com/en/news-archive/brembo-brake-master-cylinder), [Brembo on braking torque](https://www.brembo.com/en/news-archive/5-factors-that-make-a-brembo-braking-system-unbeatable). These sources establish mechanisms; dimensions and numerical compliance are authored teaching assumptions.

Five model tests currently pass: port/gap behavior, continuous volume and air balance, force/displacement and integrated work, kinetic-energy conversion, and direct-seek reconstruction. The bilingual route and 177.0-second film are integrated. Desktop review adjusted the caliper angle, translucent end caps, mounting ears, linked rod endpoints and continuous metal finish. Phone review checked English 320 px input extremes, no horizontal overflow, removal of the WebGL canvas in 2D mode and complete reset. A final gas chapter now starts with an uncompressed pocket, squeezes again and compares pressure with a gas-free circuit at the same stroke. Both recordings were regenerated for the revised wording; all 16 chapter/language pairs passed independent ASR (minimum similarity 0.9006). Complete listening, continuous visual viewing and physical-phone performance remain pending.
