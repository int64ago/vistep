# Myopia-control spectacles: rays first, evidence separately

## Question and art direction

The user asked for children's myopia-control lenses. Use DIMS as one explicitly named example, not a mechanism common to every commercial design. Follow a single distant point: uncorrected focus before the receiving plane; distance correction returns its clear focus; an admitted annular lenslet contributes a second, forward focus. The turning point distinguishes correcting blur from evidence of slower subsequent progression.

Original art is an ivory optical bench, transparent equivalent-eye envelope, blue correcting rays and warm amber lenslet rays. The lens-surface chapter gives the sampled array the main view; the close chapter enlarges the actual calculated intersections. Clinical evidence receives a separately composed, honest-scale bar comparison. This is deliberately a ray diagram, not a medically accurate 3D anatomical eye. Phone labels stay in CSS pixels outside the SVG, the front lens/detail stack is separately arranged, and the focal close view remains the main object.

## Seven planned windows

Seven 23-second windows plan 161 seconds; recordings determine the final shared timeline. Each chapter changes the observation: (1) receiving plane moves behind a fixed uncorrected focus; (2) negative-power correction aligns the focus; (3) front surface exposes the 9 mm clear zone and 1.03 mm lenslets; (4) changing incidence admits the selected bundle; (5) actual computed focal separation is enlarged; (6) a two-year randomized-trial comparison replaces the optical view; (7) return to the whole ray path connects both roles and explicitly excludes growth prediction.

Speech names objects rather than positions so the same references work on phones. No private clock, WebGL, audio renderer or Worker is introduced; the component follows the shared film clock. Reverse seeks reconstruct the same pure state. Exploration changes refractive mismatch, lens kind, incidence and added power; it never outputs an individual treatment forecast.

## Independent model and boundaries

`myopia-lens.ts` propagates height and reduced angle in metres through a 12 mm spectacle-to-eye gap, a 60 D equivalent eye and n=1.336 interior. For myopic mismatch M, the image plane is n/(60−M); spectacle power −M/(1−0.012M) includes vertex correction. A local lenslet adds −A(h−h₀) to reduced angle. The same rays determine pupil admission, focal intersections and the close-view distance. Blocked rays stop at the iris.

The traced plane contains the optical axis and selected lenslet centre, using radial height for the circular pupil. This is paraxial thin-lens, flat-image-plane optics, including an approximate 18° off-axis teaching view. It omits anatomical surfaces, aberrations, accommodation, pupil dynamics, binocular vision, biological growth and product fitting. Equivalent principal-plane distance is not clinical axial length. The hexagonal layout is sampled, not a manufacturing map. The small surface section is illustrative; no surface curvature is claimed as a real manufactured lens prescription.

The clinical panel consistently uses Table 2 follow-up completers from Lam et al.: 79 DIMS and 81 single-vision participants. Two-year axial growth means are 0.21±0.02 mm and 0.53±0.03 mm; errors are SE. Refraction means are −0.38±0.06 D and −0.93±0.06 D. Do not mix these with the separately adjusted abstract statistics or compute percentages across different populations. Both groups grew. No slider affects these measured group endpoints.
