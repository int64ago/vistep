# Single-slit diffraction

[简体中文](../zh-CN/examples/diffraction-brief.md)

## Causal argument

Follow field contributions from one illuminated slit to one screen point. The turning point is a dark fringe created by cancellation, not by the absence of light at the aperture.

## Direction and chapters

A pale optical drawing combines aperture paths, a complex-vector sum and a screen intensity profile. Eight chapters reveal the slit, sum contributions, pair cancellation at the first minimum, square the resultant field, vary width, vary wavelength, test distance and examine the zero equation’s limits. Phones select the vector or aperture view needed by that chapter.

## Model and limits

Uniform monochromatic scalar Fraunhofer model: E/E₀ = sinc β, β = πa sinθ/λ. Finite strip contributions are integrated exactly and checked against independent quadrature. Each profile normalizes its own central peak; it cannot compare throughput. Predictions are withheld when full-width Fresnel number exceeds 0.1 or width is below 20 wavelengths. These are conservative teaching guards, not physical discontinuities. The final ratio experiment is algebra, not subwavelength electrodynamics.
