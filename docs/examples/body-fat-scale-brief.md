# Body fat scale — measure a signal, then make an estimate

[简体中文](../zh-CN/examples/body-fat-scale-brief.md) · [Production guide](../creating-a-scene.md)

The initial intuition is that the scale somehow sees fat. Track one invented subject, the same four electrodes, and the same current/voltage measurement. The turning point changes only tissue water while actual fat mass and dry fat-free tissue stay fixed: estimated fat mass moves. Total weight and therefore the true fat percentage also change. The topic provides no personal measurement, diagnosis or treatment guidance.

The original composition starts with an inspectable glass scale and bare feet, then separates two physical chains. Four grounded metal load-cell frames, elastic beams, bonded foils and contacting standoffs support the glass. A separate PCB holds an AC source, voltage input and real protruding package pins. Warm current conductors close through the source, front-foot current pads, equivalent legs and lower trunk. Blue voltage conductors terminate at the two heel pads and a separate high-impedance input. The later medium changes to paired waveforms, a tissue-equivalent circuit, a sequential estimate, and two mass compositions. It is not a repeated object-and-slider layout.

## Primary evidence and boundaries

- [TI AFE4300](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/afe4300.pdf) distinguishes load-cell/bridge and body-composition measurement chains. [TI SBAA290](https://www.ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SBAA290) supplies the strain-bridge principle. The model does not copy a product's mechanical stiffness or weighing calibration.
- [TI SBAA562](https://www.ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SBAA562), [Analog Devices' four-wire impedance explanation](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/technical-articles/jack-of-all-trades-in-impedance-measurement.html), and [MAX30009](https://www.analog.com/en/products/max30009.html) distinguish current injection from high-input-impedance voltage sensing. Separate contacts reduce contact effects; their influence is not claimed to vanish.
- [Analog Devices' equivalent circuit and I/Q explanation](https://www.analog.com/en/resources/analog-dialogue/articles/adopting-a-better-bioimpedance-afe.html) supports extracellular resistance in parallel with a membrane-capacitance/intracellular-resistance branch. The phase of the modeled impedance is negative; the UI reports positive voltage-lag magnitude and signed negative reactance.
- [Lukaski et al., 1985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3984933/) studied a particular adult male sample using a tetrapolar arrangement. The height-squared/resistance relationship is borrowed as a teaching form. Neither its coefficients nor accuracy are transferred to every foot scale.
- [Wang, Deurenberg and Heymsfield, 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10865761/) discusses fat-free-mass hydration around 0.73 and its variation. This demonstration explicitly fixes 0.73 as an assumption, not a universal individual constant.
- [Tanita's drinking-water explanation](https://support.tanita.eu/support/solutions/articles/60000690203-how-will-drinking-water-affect-my-body-composition-readings-) distinguishes newly ingested water from already distributed tissue fluid. Changing model tissue water is not an immediate prediction after drinking.

## Independent models and actual contacts

The invented reference is 1.70 m, 40.15 L water, 14.85 kg dry fat-free tissue and 15 kg fat, giving 70 kg total mass. Water converts approximately at 1 kg/L. The RC reference uses Rₑ=750 Ω, Rᵢ=1000 Ω and Cₘ=8 nF. Both resistances scale by reference water/current water; capacitance is fixed. The independent complex impedance is `Rₑ || (Rᵢ + 1/jωCₘ)`. Ionic fluid conduction and capacitive membrane response are represented, without a heterogeneous current-density solver or separate tissue reconstruction.

At 50 kHz, R=444.37 Ω, X=−69.49 Ω, |Z|=449.77 Ω, phase=−8.89°. The disclosed source is 250 μA RMS, producing 112.44 mV RMS. These values are teaching inputs, not instructions for a safe circuit. Waveforms share two cycles and have independently labeled amplitude ranges; the voltage range expands when exploration needs it.

The uncalibrated coefficient `k = 40.15 × Rreference / 1.70²` reproduces only that invented reference. At 50 kHz, `TBW=k h²/R`, `FFM=TBW/0.73`, and `fat=weight−FFM`. Other frequencies keep their electrical response but provide no fat estimate. Open current contacts give no measured impedance or fat output; weight remains available. Fat-free mass includes water and dry tissue, not just muscle.

Four reactions balance gravity and both first moments. A raised right foot shifts the pressure centre without changing mass. The weighing chapter separately applies zero→full gravitational load while the subject's mass/water stay fixed. Model strain is force×3 μstrain/N, gauge factor 2, bridge excitation 1.7 V. The visible gauge ruler exaggerates its extension 1000×. Opposed bridge resistances and differential voltage derive from that same applied load. Glass, standoff, beam and grounded frame stay in contact; transparency exposes the interior instead of lifting the loaded deck into an impossible gap.

World illustration units are 10 cm. Rounded parts, shared foot ellipsoids, electrodes, PCB ports and electrical polylines share one geometry. Toe and heel tangencies contact the correct pads. The current source's closing segment lies inside its actual package. Pin surfaces meet their packages. Slowed signal markers use an integrated phase, `fraction−0.016 cos(2π phase)`, so their pathwise velocity reverses with `I(t)∝sin(2π phase)`. They are signal identifiers, not calculated charge trajectories or propagation speeds.
