# Gyroscope — torque turns angular momentum

Owner: `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Thin steel rotor on a horizontal massless shaft, with a real fixed pivot, pedestal and support connection. Independent three-dimensional composition, with a top-view angular-momentum construction when direction is the key evidence. No generic dashboard.

Misconceptions: a spinning rotor cancels gravity; torque points in the direction of the force; faster spin must mean faster precession; a steady-precession formula describes release from rest. Distinction from flywheel: this scene changes angular-momentum direction under an external torque, rather than buffering energy through a speed change. Distinction from angular-momentum: the support-plus-gravity system has a nonzero external torque.

Verified primary sources: OpenStax University Physics 11.4, https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/11-4-precession-of-a-gyroscope (vector torque, precession, approximate fast-spin derivation); MIT 8.01SC chapter 22, https://live.ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01sc-classical-mechanics-fall-2016/mit8_01scs22_chapter22.pdf (three-dimensional rotations); MIT release demonstration transcript https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-01sc-classical-mechanics-fall-2016/bf05a2580eca7a3a92d0a770e608af76_5oLLnCGStUc.pdf (release can include nutation). Original model derives the exact horizontal steady branch rather than extending the approximate formula to arbitrary initial conditions.

Model: mass1 kg, disc radius.18 m, center lever arm.22 m, axial spin ±120 or240 rad/s. Axial moment I3=mR²/2; transverse pivot moment I1=m(R²/4+l²). Horizontal axis n=(cosφ,0,−sinφ); Ω=mgl/(I3ω), φ=Ωt. Angular momentum L=I3ω n+I1Ω Y. Its full derivative equals r×mg exactly. Support reaction includes vertical mg and inward m l Ω². Initial angular velocity already includes the steady precession. Comparing independently prepared steady states; no claim of release dynamics, friction, bearing ratings or stabilization at zero spin.

Six causal shots: visible steady horizontal precession; gravity, pivot reaction and perpendicular torque; top-view L direction changing tangentially; double spin halves precession over equal time; reverse spin reverses precession; zero gravity removes torque and leaves the prepared axis direction fixed while spin continues. Persistent state label states prepared horizontal precession, and detailed assumptions also appear in the article.
