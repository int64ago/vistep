## Misconception and distinct addition

An open pressure node is not a fixed air particle. A stopped cylinder’s second allowed mode is not twice its fundamental. End correction is an approximation with a defined boundary regime, not a universal tuning constant. Prerequisite: sound as longitudinal pressure and particle motion. Adds air-column boundary selection to the transverse-string `standing-wave` and uses `acoustic-reflection` pressure/velocity relations. Artwork is the equivalent resonator segment, not a pretend complete flue-generation model.

## Art and causal sequence

A softly lit dark-green cutaway cylindrical resonator with metal sidewalls, a real cap when stopped, pressure shading and longitudinal particle identities. A shared-pressure profile stands beside the column, with fixed pressure-node circles. No decorative upward travelling sine is substituted for a standing longitudinal mode.

1. Open fundamental: pressure zero at ends, particle motion large there.
2. Quarter-cycle: pressure/displacement fall while velocity arrows grow.
3. Same length, close top: cap fixes displacement and gives quarter-wave fundamental.
4. Next stopped mode is the third harmonic, with new internal nodes and absent even frequency slots.
5. Return fundamental, grow actual geometric length on a fixed world scale and lower frequency.
6. Open both ends and enlarge lower mouth; slowly add correction and move effective pressure node outside the rim. Phase held fixed so the node shift is clear.
