# Markov chain — author brief

Misconceptions: stationary means individuals stop; every stationary distribution attracts all starts; one short trajectory has exact population frequencies; a reducible chain has no stationary distribution. Different from Bayes: forward propagation with fixed conditional transition rates rather than conditioning on new evidence. Different from future random-walk scene: probability transport and invariant distributions, not spatial diffusion.

Art direction: a probability loom. Pale blue A and warm parchment B send four curved ribbons whose widths are actual pᵢPᵢⱼ; native HTML marginal percentages and a conditional matrix remain readable.
