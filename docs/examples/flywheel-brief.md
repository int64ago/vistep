# Flywheel — buffer energy, do not create it

Owner: `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Original rotor face plus an accurately scaled axial mass section, making radial mass redistribution and constant mass visible. Both views share annular geometry; the axial thickness increases when mass moves to a narrower outer ring. Lightweight hub/spokes connect the ring but their inertia is explicitly neglected. The decisive evidence is a torque mismatch and matching kinetic-energy change, not a decorative spinning wheel.

Misconception: flywheels guarantee constant speed, create extra energy, or replace a governor under a lasting load change. Prerequisites: faster/slower, input/output, stored energy. Distinction from centrifugal-governor: this lesson buffers cyclic energy mechanically, without changing source input in response to speed.
