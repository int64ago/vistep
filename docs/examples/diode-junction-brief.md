# PN junction: fixed ions, variable barrier

Owner `/root/hundred_mechanisms`. Misconceptions: the depletion region is empty of all charge; a silicon diode has a universal 0.7 V opening threshold; built-in potential is available as an external battery. Prerequisites: electric field, potential, positive and negative charge. Adds microscopic depletion electrostatics and the terminal-load boundary to the existing macroscopic circuits.

Primary claims and sources (checked 2026-10-18): MIT 6.012 Fall 2009 lecture 4, p–n junction electrostatics, for charge neutrality, Poisson equation and abrupt-junction depletion width; lecture 6 / Spring 2009 lecture 15, junction I–V, for the ideal Shockley law and limits. Source landing pages in packet. Silicon 300 K, ε=11.7ε₀, Na=10²²/m³, Nd=3×10²²/m³, ni=10¹⁶/m³, chosen device saturation current 1 pA. ni and Is fixed at this temperature; no temperature intervention or claim that Is follows from doping alone.

Hero is a spatial cross-section with fixed ion symbols in the depletion layer, majority-carrier symbols outside, an electric-field arrow directed n→p and a physically aligned potential profile. Negative p-side space-charge width is three times positive n-side width; total charge balances. Ion marks remain at fixed coordinates as boundaries move; a three-to-one spacing ratio represents the doping ratio and exposes equal numbers in the two depletion halves. Symbols depict charge type and coarse density, not literal atomic positions. Mobile carrier symbols do not assert single-particle trajectories. Equilibrium diffusion and drift balance, but microscopic dynamics are not simulated.

Six chapters: unbiased cross-section and built-in field; signed space-charge density revealing equal opposite areas; forward bias reduces barrier and depletion width; 0.45→0.60 V exposes continuous exponential current without a hard threshold; reverse bias widens the depletion region with 1 pA model reverse current; a 100 Ω resistor shares the terminal voltage and limits forward growth.

Model separately computes junction voltage by monotone load-line bisection, then Vbi−Vj, W, xp/xn, potential and field from Poisson. Supported bare junction Vj≤0.60 V, terminal V≤1 V with 100 Ω; rejects barriers≤0.08 V. Ideal diffusion current, no breakdown, generation-recombination leakage, high injection, self-heating, or switching storage. Width and current curves are steady configurations, not the transient evolution of contacting crystals.
