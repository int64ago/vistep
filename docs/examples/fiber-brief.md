# Optical fiber — production brief

[简体中文](../zh-CN/examples/fiber-brief.md)

Part of the [50-scene expansion](../expansion-50.md). Follow a pulse through a glass core. The turning point is that confinement comes from the refractive-index boundary and incidence angle, not from metallic mirror walls.

The visual language is a luminous axial section, a magnified dielectric interface and an arrival-time plot. It deliberately omits a decorative 3D cable. Phone views use a shorter, explicitly labeled section with unchanged cross-sectional scale; boundary diagrams are recomposed at the available width.

The eight chapters reveal core/cladding/coating, split a ray at a glass–air interface, cross the critical angle, return to a real index contrast, exceed the acceptance angle, remove the contrast, compare modal arrival times and transmit a short bit sequence. Each change has a computed consequence. Optional controls select the experiment and vary entry angle, interface incidence or cladding index.

The model implements Snell's law, unpolarized Fresnel intensity, actual wall intersections, reflected and escaped branch power, step-index meridional path lengths and delayed bit reception. Powers are normalized inside the entrance face. Geometry uses a 25 μm core radius, n=1.48 and n=1.46; it is a teaching fiber, not a specific product. Full-wave modes, evanescent fields, bend loss, polarization evolution, material dispersion, coating optics and real loss budgets are outside scope. Do not identify multiple ray paths with exact wave modes or use this ray model to explain single-mode confinement.

Sources: [OpenStax: total internal reflection](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/1-4-total-internal-reflection), [Corning: fiber terminology](https://www.corning.com/optical-communications/worldwide/en/home/products/fiber/optical-fiber-resource-center/glossary-of-terms.html). The 180-second bilingual film and articles are integrated. Desktop and 320 px key frames and controls have been reviewed; full playback and listening remain outstanding. Model tests do not establish a finished lesson.
