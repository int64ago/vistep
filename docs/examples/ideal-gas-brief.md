## Misconception and distinct addition

Pressure is impulse flux, not particles piling against a wall; temperature affects impulse and rate; gauge zero differs from vacuum; heavy atoms at fixed T/n/V do not raise ideal pressure. Prerequisites: bouncing particles and average force. Extends `brownian-motion` from finite collision imbalance to wall pressure. Future `adiabatic-compression` must not inherit this scene's isothermal state comparison as a process.
