The integrated review is recorded below. Source and numerical checks, browser observations, playback and listening are distinct evidence types. This is a pre-publication review, not a deployment record.

## Confirmed changes

- Corrected the bucket linkage so cylinder extension curls the actual cutting edge inward and upward. Fixed link lengths, the chosen assembly branch, cylinder housing/rod overlap and shared pin positions constrain the motion. Model, 3D rendering, 2D drawing and catalog/social cover use the same mechanism geometry.
- Corrected the hydraulic section to translate a fixed-length rod with its piston. During holding, work ports are closed and the ideal variable pump destrokes. A renewed lift command then opens P–A and B–T; the overloaded state stops lifting and activates the pump-side relief path. Zero supplied flow shows no flow arrow.
- Distinguished required pressure, capped supply pressure, hydraulic input power, actuator mechanical power and relief loss. The section explicitly represents one of two boom cylinders sharing total flow equally.
- Updated the bilingual articles, narration and catalog entries. Manufacturer sources support cylinder direction, force/flow relationships and the distinction between main relief and dedicated load holding; source links and their limits are in the brief.

The model assumes planar rigid links, quasi-static loads, identical boom cylinders, equal flow division and zero return backpressure. Thus `Ftotal = 2pA` and `v = Qtotal/(2A)` for ideal extension. At relief, pump input can remain positive while actuator mechanical power is zero. The chosen 24 MPa ceiling is a teaching limit, not a lifting chart. Soil forces, inertia, leakage, oil compression, structural stress and tipping are omitted. Manual pose controls compare static states rather than commanding integrated machine motion.

## Evidence limits

Physical-phone performance and native listening to both complete recordings have **not been measured**; the current review environment cannot provide those two evidence types. Browser viewport overrides, rendered stills, finite parameter sweeps, successful playback and MLX transcription cannot substitute for them. The remaining gaps are continuous human visual watching, native listening, physical-device performance, and the browser-only failure/media-preference cases identified above; automated results are not presented as substitutes.
