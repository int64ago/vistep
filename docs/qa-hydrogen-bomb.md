# Hydrogen bomb review — 2026-09-12

[简体中文](zh-CN/qa-hydrogen-bomb.md) · [Production brief](examples/hydrogen-bomb-brief.md) · [Review index](README.md#review-records)

This is the pre-publication review of the hydrogen bomb (thermonuclear fusion) scene, produced on parent commit `84d3ab8` together with the atomic bomb scene. It records what was checked, by whom, and what remains untested. It does not claim a successful deployment or a published-site verification.

The scene is educational plasma and nuclear physics only: Coulomb repulsion and classical turning points, binding energy of D, T and ⁴He, the Maxwell tail multiplied by tunnelling into the Gamow peak, Bosch–Hale reactivity against temperature, why only a fission explosion supplies the temperature and radiation for a large mass of fuel, lithium-6 breeding of tritium, and the three confinement routes on a Lawson chart. It goes no further than the public statement that a fission primary's radiation heats and compresses separately placed fusion fuel: no geometry, dimensions, materials arrangement, yields or fuel quantities. An independent reviewer checked every scene file against that boundary and found nothing beyond it.

## Roles

The scene owner (Claude Fable 5.1, a forked worker) wrote the model, director, component, styles, articles, cover and briefs. The integration owner (Claude Fable 5.1, main session) registered the scene, produced the recordings, ran the browser review, forwarded findings and accepted repairs. A separate read-only reviewer (Claude Fable 5.1, fresh context) recomputed the physics independently. Owner self-review is labelled as such below.

## Evidence limits

- Neither full recording received native listening, and neither film was continuously watched. ASR cannot establish pronunciation, naturalness or word-level synchronization.
- Physical-phone performance was not measured.
- Reduced motion, browser-background hiding, offscreen pause, blocked autoplay recovery and audio-asset failure rely on the shared player and were not re-exercised for this scene beyond the autoplay prompt appearing in headless runs.
- The scene uses SVG only, no WebGL and no Worker, so the runtime WebGL fallback does not apply.
- This record ends before publication. Pushing `main` triggers CI deployment; CI success and the live site's behaviour are outside this evidence.
