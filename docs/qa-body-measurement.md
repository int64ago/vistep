## Scientific scope

The scale distinguishes strain-gauge weighing from four-electrode bioimpedance measurement. Its impedance, phase and equivalent tissue circuit feed an explicitly uncalibrated teaching estimate. Hydration comparisons retain the same fat and dry lean mass while total mass changes with water. The scene does not estimate a visitor’s body composition.

The ear thermometer follows passive thermal radiation from tissue to the detector. A geometric acceptance model chooses the first surface intersected by each weighted ray. An ideal finite-band Planck integral, sensor reference temperature and calibration feed the displayed radiometric temperature. Its anatomy, response and calibration are teaching choices, not a manufacturer’s clinical algorithm or a prediction of core temperature.

Discovery places the scale in Electricity & magnetism (suggested starting age 12) and the thermometer in Light & waves (age 10). These ages are editorial guidance. Both have everyday and technical aliases in both languages.
