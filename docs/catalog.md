# Exploration catalog

[简体中文](zh-CN/catalog.md) · [Documentation](README.md)

The catalog below follows registered explorations and measured film lengths. Each published exploration pairs bilingual reading with a directed film and static recordings. Acceptance evidence distinguishes numerical checks, browser observations, transcription and remaining review gaps. Earlier work is documented in the [twenty-scene review](qa-twenty-explorations.md), [body-measurement review](qa-body-measurement.md) and [collection expansion record](expansion-50.md). A local review record does not establish a production deployment.

Topics follow their main explanation. Heat and fluids have their own group; Earth and astronomy covers planetary and celestial phenomena. Jellyfish belongs to Heat & fluids because its film tracks water displacement, vortices and thrust. Weapon subjects share a dedicated context group. Related links and bilingual search aliases retain connections across fields.

### Suggested learning paths

These optional sequences cross subject categories. The site gives a reason for each step and a starting-concept guide; age is editorial guidance, not a content rating. Film lengths below round up to the next second, consistently with the three-minute filter.

- **How a photograph takes shape** — Start with: light travels, and a photo contains pixels. [Camera lenses](../src/content/en/camera-lens.mdx) → [Aperture and depth of field](../src/content/en/aperture.mdx) → [Bayer sensor](../src/content/en/bayer-sensor.mdx) → [Rolling shutter](../src/content/en/rolling-shutter.mdx) → [JPEG compression](../src/content/en/jpeg.mdx).
- **Different views of a waveform** — Start with: period, amplitude and frequency. [Wave interference](../src/content/en/wave-interference.mdx) → [Beats](../src/content/en/beats.mdx) → [Standing wave](../src/content/en/standing-wave.mdx) → [Sampling & aliasing](../src/content/en/sampling-aliasing.mdx) → [Fourier decomposition](../src/content/en/fourier-transform.mdx).
- **How a webpage reaches your phone** — Start with: a network has senders, receivers and intermediate nodes. [Loading a webpage](../src/content/en/network.mdx) → [Packet routing](../src/content/en/packet-routing.mdx) → [TCP reliability](../src/content/en/tcp-reliability.mdx) → [Congestion control](../src/content/en/congestion-control.mdx).
- **Converting and delivering electrical energy** — Start with: voltage, current and stored energy are different quantities. [Electric generator](../src/content/en/electric-generator.mdx) → [Electrical transformer](../src/content/en/transformer-electric.mdx) → [Rectifier](../src/content/en/rectifier.mdx) → [Buck converter](../src/content/en/buck-converter.mdx).
