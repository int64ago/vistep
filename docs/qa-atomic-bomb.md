# Atomic bomb review — 2026-09-12

[简体中文](zh-CN/qa-atomic-bomb.md) · [Production brief](examples/atomic-bomb-brief.md) · [Review index](README.md#review-records)

This is the pre-publication review of the atomic bomb (fission chain reaction) scene, produced on parent commit `84d3ab8` together with the hydrogen bomb scene. It records what was checked, by whom, and what remains untested. It does not claim a successful deployment or a published-site verification.

The scene is educational nuclear physics only: nucleus deformation and scission, the binding-energy curve, generation-by-generation multiplication, leakage versus production in a sphere measured in mean free paths, the reactor/bomb comparison through delayed neutrons, and expansion-driven shutdown. It contains no critical mass in kilograms, no reflector or tamper, no assembly method and no physical dimensions; all lengths are in mean free paths and the collision ratios are declared illustrative. An independent reviewer grepped every scene file for such content and found none.

## Evidence limits

- Neither full recording received native listening, and neither film was continuously watched. ASR cannot establish pronunciation, naturalness or word-level synchronization.
- Physical-phone performance was not measured; the walk chapter redraws a 2D canvas each frame, and its frame rate on low-end phones is unknown.
- Reduced motion, browser-background hiding, offscreen pause, blocked autoplay recovery and audio-asset failure rely on the shared player and were not re-exercised for this scene beyond the autoplay prompt appearing in headless runs.
- The scene uses a 2D canvas and SVG, no WebGL and no Worker, so the runtime WebGL fallback does not apply.
- This record ends before publication. Pushing `main` triggers CI deployment; CI success and the live site's behaviour are outside this evidence.
