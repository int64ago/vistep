<h2 id="understand">Understand the principle</h2>

This scene covers unit cells, quasicrystals and structural measurement on PEP Selective Compulsory 2 pages 73–76, plus organic X-ray diffraction on Selective Compulsory 3 page 20. Molecular versus covalent-network bonding has its own scene.

Corner, edge and face sites neighbor 8, 4 and 2 cells, giving counting weights 1/8, 1/4 and 1/2; an internal site counts as 1. Copper’s **conventional face-centred cubic cell** counts 4 atoms. A conventional cell need not be the smallest-volume primitive cell. The sodium chloride option separately constructs corner/face chloride and edge/central sodium sites, counting 4 of each. Illustrative sphere sizes are not measured ionic radii.

Aperiodic order need not have ordinary three-dimensional lattice translation periodicity. A finite Penrose patch is generated by golden-ratio Robinson-triangle subdivision and uses its vertices as equal scatterers. It is not an alloy reconstruction. The translated copy fails to align for the chosen shift; a finite comparison does not itself prove aperiodicity. See [Frank’s substitution-tiling review](https://pages.vassar.edu/nafrank/files/2012/08/substitutions.pdf) and the [IUCr crystal definition](https://dictionary.iucr.org/Crystal). Lacking an ordinary periodic cell does not automatically mean being amorphous.

<h2 id="try">Try it yourself</h2>

Every pattern computes F(q)=Σexp(2πi q·r) from current planar coordinates, then |F|²/N². A fixed logarithmic display makes weak signals visible. Finite windows broaden peaks and introduce edge effects. This planar calculation is not an experimental Ewald-sphere section through a three-dimensional crystal, and it omits alloy-specific scattering factors.

Periodic and disordered comparisons both contain 121 identical points. Seeded random positions retain a minimum separation but do not reproduce the Si–O network of silica glass. Both panels use the same coherent sum and brightness mapping. Real peak interpretation also depends on mixed phases, crystallite size, orientation, defects and instrumental broadening.

A separate powder example uses 125 equal three-dimensional scatterers. Pair distances enter the Debye orientational average I(q)=[N+2Σsin(qrᵢⱼ)/(qrᵢⱼ)]/N². The left/right half-rings and two curves share the periodic/disordered intensities and colours. Relative inverse-length q is not a fabricated SiO₂ 2θ measurement or a strictly linear detector radius. Equal scattering, single scattering and omitted absorption/background are simplifications. See [IUCr’s structure-factor notes](https://www.iucr.org/what-we-do/education/pamphlets/introduction-calculation-of-structure-factors).

<h2 id="deeper">Go deeper</h2>

The organic fit uses a constructed planar acetic-acid heavy-atom example. Carbon and oxygen weights 6 and 8 only approximate zero-angle electron counts; angular scattering-factor variation is omitted. Composition, three other heavy-atom positions, an 8 Å periodic box and reference reflections are supplied. One C—O distance is optimized from 1.75 Å toward 1.21 Å using finite-difference gradients and backtracking. Playback reconstructs the computed optimization history. Pale circles show known reference positions for teaching; they are not an electron-density map directly inverted from measured intensities.

Experiments normally provide intensities without all phases. Solving phases, identifying symmetry, using chemical information and refinement remain necessary. A noiseless single-parameter example with most coordinates given does not establish general uniqueness or absolute configuration.

The artemisinin model uses the published coordinate and connectivity tables for Form I_1 in [Fetah et al.’s crystallographic study](https://journals.iucr.org/b/issues/2026/03/00/aw5104/index.html): 296 K and 0.3 GPa, with orthorhombic cell lengths a=6.2555 Å, b=9.1513 Å and c=23.860 Å. Fifteen carbon and five oxygen positions from one molecule are converted to Cartesian coordinates. Hydrogen, neighboring molecules and displacement ellipsoids are omitted; spheres are not electron-cloud boundaries. Independent geometry checks reproduce the paper’s O1—O2 distance 1.492(14) Å, O5—C12 distance 1.194(11) Å and C4—O1—O2 angle 109.1(9)°. Displayed calculations cannot improve the refinement’s precision.

These 2026 coordinates demonstrate a verifiable link from coordinates to geometry; they are not historical discovery measurements. The paper discusses light-atom and absolute-structure limitations, so this dataset alone is not presented as proof of absolute configuration. Published artemisinin geometry, a constructed acetic-acid fit and planar point-scattering models remain distinct kinds of evidence.
