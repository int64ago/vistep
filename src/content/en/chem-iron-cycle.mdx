<section id="understand">
## Separate the causes of colour changes

Fe³⁺ forms red thiocyanate complexes, while the Fe²⁺ control lacks the same response. This is solution colour, not a red precipitate.

For redox conversion, iron first reduces Fe³⁺: 2Fe³⁺ + Fe → 3Fe²⁺. After reaction, thiocyanate tests the solution; then the supernatant is transferred away from residual iron. Chlorine water subsequently oxidizes Fe²⁺: 2Fe²⁺ + Cl₂ → 2Fe³⁺ + 2Cl⁻, restoring the red response. Separation prevents leftover reductant from interfering.

The equilibrium experiment retains three portions of the same starting mixture: a unchanged, b with added iron and c with added SCN⁻. Consuming Fe³⁺ reduces coloured complex in b, while additional ligand increases it in c. Both observations require comparison with a.

</section>
<section id="try">
## Track oxidation state and ligand distribution together

Reduction does more than relabel ions: dissolved iron from the metal is included, so final Fe²⁺ can exceed the initial Fe³⁺ amount. The supernatant arrow represents material transfer, not a tube connection. Oxidation then restores Fe³⁺ and its colour response.

In the equilibrium and coordination views, changing SCN⁻ redistributes free Fe³⁺, mono- and bis-ligand complexes. Iron remains formally +3 on coordination. Binding and redox are distinct changes. Colour depth is qualitative and is not an absorbance calibration.

</section>
<section id="deeper">
<details>
<summary>Textbook shorthand and a more detailed species model</summary>

Elective Book 1 uses Fe³⁺ + 3SCN⁻ ⇌ Fe(SCN)₃ as a simplified equilibrium expression. Actual species depend on concentration, ionic strength, acidity and time. The model uses log K₁=2.85 and log K₂=1.51 from a [2017 original study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ica.2017.06.017), extrapolated to zero ionic strength at 25 °C, for ideal dilute Fe(SCN)²⁺ and Fe(SCN)₂⁺. The textbook shorthand is not treated as the only microscopic species under every condition.

Bisection solves the ligand-total equation while preserving both iron and SCN totals. The redox and coordination views use illustrative totals of 0.0025 mol/L iron and 0.0075 mol/L SCN. The three-tube equilibrium comparison starts with 0.0025 mol/L iron and a lower 0.001 mol/L SCN so the ligand-induced change remains visible; this comparison is not a numerical reconstruction of the textbook mixture. Added SCN is illustrative and is not a conversion of its four drops into volume. Added-volume effects, hydrolysis, higher complexes, activities and long-term instability are omitted. The [Delaware experiment](https://udel.edu/~dougr/Exp5.pdf) also distinguishes activity constants from concentration quotients.

</details>
