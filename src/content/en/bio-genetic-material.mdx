<h2 id="understand">Build the argument in stages</h2>

Griffith compared live R, live S, heat-killed S and a mixture. Live S recovered from the mixed group produced descendants retaining S traits. This supported a heritable change caused by a transforming principle, without identifying DNA or implying that killed S cells revived.

The textbook summarizes Avery and colleagues through selective treatments: activity remains after protease, RNase or lipase but is lost after DNase. Combined with purification and chemical characterization, this points to DNA. Component symbols do not imply equal quantities. Culture symbols do not report measured cell counts or transformation frequencies.

Hershey and Chase grew T2 through isotope-labeled E. coli and used separate phage batches to infect unlabeled hosts. Sulfur-35 mainly traced coat protein; phosphorus-32 mainly traced DNA. Blending, centrifugation and progeny assays distinguished their dominant destinations.

<h2 id="deeper">Predominantly does not mean completely</h2>

<details>
<summary>Textbook conclusions and research qualifications</summary>

The textbook enzyme panel summarizes a research logic rather than reproducing every procedure from 1944. The later DNase investigation is documented by McCarty and Avery in 1946. This model does not calculate purification yields, enzyme kinetics, recipient competence or animal disease progression.

The 1952 abstract reports shear-induced release of approximately 75% of phage sulfur and 15% of phosphorus. Progeny contained less than 1% of parental sulfur radioactivity and 30% or more of parental phosphorus. These are different measurements from absolute fraction purity or the number of labeled offspring. High and low signals in the film reproduce qualitative textbook findings; icon counts and colored areas are not detector readings.

Coat and DNA geometry, scale and trajectories are explanatory. Diamond and circular markers retain their material identities; the actual isotope batches are separate. The findings support a hereditary role for DNA in T2 without excluding unmeasured trace constituents or proving that every virus has a DNA genome. The textbook next discusses RNA viruses.

The no-blending comparison starts a fresh sample with the same four cell and coat identities; it does not reverse an already separated tube. Progeny glyphs are unweighted population symbols: no subset is colored to stand for the measured radioactivity fraction. The denominator remains total parental radioactivity. The [1952 primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1085/jgp.36.1.39), printed p. 55, reports these quantities and its limits. The [1946 DNase study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19871520/) supplies the later selective-inactivation evidence.
