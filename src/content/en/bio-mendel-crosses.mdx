<h2 id="understand">Control parentage before explaining offspring</h2>

Remove stamens from the maternal flower before they mature, retaining its pistil. Bag the flower, apply chosen pollen and bag again. Controlled pollen origin makes offspring interpretable.

A pure tall DD parent crossed with a short dd parent yields Dd offspring. Selfing Dd predicts DD : Dd : dd = 1 : 2 : 1 and, with complete dominance, a 3 : 1 phenotype ratio. The recessive factor never disappears. A dd tester predicts 1 : 1 and tests the inferred gamete composition.

<h2 id="try">Compare probabilities with observations</h2>

- Track each ball from its own bucket and back before the next draw. Each bucket has 10 D and 10 d balls.
- Change sample size or random sequence to compare frequency fluctuations. These are simulations, not Mendel’s measured data.
- Change parental genotypes or swap their roles; gamete probabilities rebuild the matrix.
- Compare double-heterozygote selfing and the double-recessive test cross, checking seed color and shape.

<h2 id="deeper">Equal-probability outcomes do not guarantee exact sample ratios</h2>

<details>
<summary>Observed counts and model assumptions</summary>

For one trait, F2 counts are 787 tall and 277 short; test-cross counts are 87 and 79. Two-trait F2 counts, ordered yellow-round, green-round, yellow-wrinkled and green-wrinkled, are 315, 108, 101 and 32. Test crosses use a different order: yellow-round, yellow-wrinkled, green-round and green-wrinkled. With F1 as mother the counts are 31, 27, 26 and 26; with F1 as father, 24, 22, 25 and 26. Category order must remain attached to its count.

Assumptions include equal segregation, complete dominance, random fertilization and no differential viability. The two-trait model additionally requires independent assortment. Linkage, viability differences or incomplete dominance can alter predictions. Custom parents change only the model; observed counts retain their original conditions. Seeded pseudorandom draws can be replayed and reconstructed after seeking. Replacement preserves bucket composition.

</details>
