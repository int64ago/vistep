<h2 id="understand">Guiding requires both a condition and a path</h2>

Radial entry into the semicircular block isolates splitting at its flat face. Curved rods and water jets apply the same condition at successive local normals. The floating-cork experiment reverses the reasoning to infer index from disappearance.

<h2 id="try">Rotate the ray, tighten the bend and change water head</h2>

Compare reflected and transmitted power across critical incidence. Tighten the rod to reveal leakage and vary outlet speed to alter both jet and optical path. Find the cork’s disappearance threshold before reading its index ratio.
