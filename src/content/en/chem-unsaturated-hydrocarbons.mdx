<section id="understand">

Decolourisation can represent different chemistry. Pages 67–68 of Compulsory Book 2 first observe ethene’s luminous, sooty flame, then test separate samples with acidic permanganate and bromine in carbon tetrachloride. Both observations invite the same question: which species is consumed, and where do its atoms go?

With sufficient acid, the manganese balance is MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 4H₂O. The view retains one Mn, four O and eight H; electron symbols denote transferred equivalents. This [acidic half-reaction](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-atoms-first/pages/16-1-balancing-oxidation-reduction-reactions) specifies the acceptor, not a unique organic oxidation endpoint.

In the net connectivity account, ethene’s two carbons remain joined, one Br attaches to each, and the original hydrogens remain. Erasing a bond line is not an elementary mechanism. Ethyne can add sufficient halogen in two stages. The triple-to-double-to-single connectivity and successive products are supported by [the author’s OpenStax textbook](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/9-3-reactions-of-alkynes-addition-of-hx-and-x2).

Addition and redox are overlapping classifications. Forming two C—Br bonds raises each carbon from −II to −I while Br goes from 0 to −I. [McMurry’s textbook](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/10-8-oxidation-and-reduction-in-organic-chemistry) explicitly includes alkene bromination among organic oxidations. The comparison here concerns connectivity and products, not an assertion that bromine addition has no electron redistribution.

Pages 37–38 of Selective Compulsory 3 add generation and purification. CaC₂ and water form C₂H₂ and Ca(OH)₂; saturated NaCl solution moderates the vigorous reaction in the textbook. CuSO₄ solution removes H₂S and other interfering impurities. Purified gas is tested separately with each reagent, then combustion is observed after the required purity check. The [University of Wisconsin carbide-lamp demonstration](https://demolab.chem.wisc.edu/general-chemistry-demonstrations-topic-page/organic-chemistry-carbide-lamp-2/) also documents the carbide–water–ethyne–combustion connection.

</section>
<section id="try">

In the electron-budget view, keep 1 mmol permanganate and sufficient acid, then reduce available electron equivalents from 5 to 4 mmol. Predict before revealing: the five-to-one account permits at most 0.8 mmol reduction, leaving 0.2 mmol permanganate. The budget is specified; it is not an ethene-volume yield prediction.

Compare reagent evidence, then change Br₂ availability in the connectivity view: one ethene unit can add one Br₂, whereas one ethyne unit can add two. Excess Br₂ retains colour instead of disappearing arbitrarily.

In preparation, reduce available Cu²⁺ and inspect remaining outlet H₂S. This explores finite purification capacity; it does not substitute for actual leak checks, purification assessment or gas-purity testing. Flame views illustrate observations rather than home operating procedures.

</section>
<section id="deeper">

<details>
<summary>Model and visual limits</summary>

Addition tracks one hydrocarbon unit, available Br₂ and progress. Every added Br₂ contributes two Br atoms without changing C or H. Ethyne retains a double bond after the first addition and a single bond after the second. Continuous progress represents bulk conversion and interpolation, not fractional molecular bonds or a real reaction path. The diagram shows connectivity, not bond-angle measurement, stereochemistry or solvent competition.

The atomic motion interpolates a net account. It does not depict oxygen release, actual proton paths or stable intermediates. H⁺ omits hydration; manganese is not assigned an unverified oxidation-state staircase. Five persistent electron IDs represent equivalents, not free aqueous electrons. Endpoint half-reaction charge is +2. The transfer ledger separately tracks a relative donor-side charge change without assigning it to a particular organic ion.

The budget view explicitly shows amounts, not fractional atoms or quantitative colour measurement. Its 8 mmol H⁺ is a sufficient net budget, not a pH calculation. Changing acidity, MnO₂ formation, specific organic endpoints and kinetics are outside the model.

Preparation starts with one CaC₂ and 2.2 H₂O units and conserves Ca, C, H and O. A separate illustrative 0.08 H₂S impurity input accompanies the gas; this is not a claimed carbide composition. Cu²⁺ + H₂S → CuS + 2H⁺ is limited by the scarcer reactant, conserving Cu and S. Other carbide impurities, pressure, transfer rates and copper acetylide preparation are not simulated.

Flame and soot are qualitative textbook observations, not a soot-yield prediction from carbon/hydrogen ratio. Complete-combustion equations are checked separately for atom balance: C₂H₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 2H₂O and C₂H₂ + 2.5O₂ → 2CO₂ + H₂O. A real sooty flame includes incomplete combustion, so not all carbon has become CO₂.

</details>
