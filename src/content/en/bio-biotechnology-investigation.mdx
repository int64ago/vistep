<h2 id="understand">An investigation records evidence, not a predetermined position</h2>

The textbook’s two social investigations concern GM-crop applications, then nearby products, labels, marketing claims and science communication. They require documentary and field evidence. An animated curve cannot replace observation, interviewing and verification.

This page uses two **fixed historical approval entries** to teach document reading and provides an initially blank personal record. Sources were checked on **2026-10-18**. The examples are not a complete current register; the fictional package and eight training records are not an actual market survey.

<h2 id="try">Make every judgment traceable</h2>

1. Consult the ministry’s [approval-information entry point](https://www.moa.gov.cn/ztzl/zjyqwgz/spxx/). Preserve publication date, title, event identifier, number, purpose, period, region and link; check later updates separately.
2. Change “processing input” to “production / planting” in the document comparator. Then change region and date to see which fields cease to match.
3. For an actual product, record exact label wording, ingredient, batch, source and date. Manufacturing claims need traceable documentation; colour, sweetness, insect damage and package graphics are insufficient.
4. Separate GM statements, non-GM claims and missing observations. Multiple photographs of one product are not additional independent products.
5. Draft an audience, question, original evidence, limits and feedback method. The tool neither publishes nor sends the plan; inputs are not uploaded or saved automatically.

<h2 id="deeper">Identity, purpose, dates and inference limits</h2>

The ministry’s October 2024 [import list](https://www.moa.gov.cn/ztzl/zjyqwgz/spxx/202410/P020241011735468898553.pdf) identifies MON87427 under certificate number 2024/065, with a listed period of 2024-10-08 to 2029-10-07. Its scope is imported processing input. The document restricts changing the purpose or environmental entry; it cannot establish permission to plant.

The corresponding [production-application list](https://www.moa.gov.cn/ztzl/zjyqwgz/spxx/202410/P020241011735470924167.pdf) identifies YK1601 papaya under number 2024/220 for the same dates, limited to suitable ecological zones in South China. The interface checks only a coarse region field, not ecological suitability.

The comparator asks whether inputs match this fixed document. Being outside its dates does not establish that no renewal exists; being inside them does not establish that nothing was amended or revoked. Safety certification, variety registration and other applicable permissions require separate verification. Articles seventeen and nineteen of the [ministry-hosted regulation](https://kjs.moa.gov.cn/zcjd/201904/t20190418_6184797.htm) distinguish these stages.

The label exercise uses the ministry-hosted [2017 revision of the labelling rules](https://kjs.moa.gov.cn/zcjd/201904/t20190418_6184800.htm) and its first catalogue. Items identify specific products, including soybean oil and cotton seed. Cotton fibre cannot be substituted for cotton seed merely because both come from cotton. Being outside the items checked here does not imply exemption from all other rules. Actual investigations must check applicable dates and official updates.

Manufacturing method, ingredient origin, final product and analytical target are distinct. As a historical example, the [FDA insulin history](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-history-exhibits/100-years-insulin) documents recombinant human insulin’s approval in 1982. This supports that product’s historical technological origin; it is not a household interview and does not imply that prepared protein contains living production bacteria.

The eight training records contain three GM statements, two non-GM claims and three missing observations. The fraction 5/8 describes records in those two observed wording categories. It is neither a GM detection rate, a safety rate nor a market share. Missing sources and dates still need to be supplied.

“Non-GM, therefore safer” moves from an origin claim to a comparative risk conclusion. Inspect the comparator, conditions, endpoints, results and applicable scope. Do not infer relative safety from a few words or invent a seller’s intentions. Science communication can identify an evidence gap while retaining unresolved questions.
