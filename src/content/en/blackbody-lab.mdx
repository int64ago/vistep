<h2 id="understand">Compare both the peak and the intensity</h2>

A small opening in a heated cavity approximates a blackbody. Spectral measurements resolve radiation at different wavelengths. Heating increases emission and moves the peak toward shorter wavelengths. Every curve uses the same intensity scale rather than being normalized to its own maximum.

<h2 id="try">Track a changing temperature</h2>

Change the furnace temperature and follow the peak marker. Compare with the 3000 K and 5000 K references. The graph spans 200–2000 nm, including ultraviolet and near infrared; colors in the apparatus identify separated rays, not the visibility of the full spectrum.
