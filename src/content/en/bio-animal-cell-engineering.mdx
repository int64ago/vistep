<h2 id="understand">Understand it</h2>

Animal-cell culture, hybridoma production and somatic-cell nuclear transfer answer different questions. Culture maintains cells; hybridomas combine persistent growth with antibody production before specificity screening; nuclear transfer introduces a somatic nuclear genome into suitable enucleated oocyte cytoplasm. Activation, development and transfer remain separate requirements. The textbook treats these processes separately on elective volume 3, pages 44–54.

Sixteen cell symbols track identity rather than an actual inoculum. In passage, eight enter each receiving vessel. The source starts with 10 mL and each receiver with 3 mL; two 2 mL transfers preserve a combined 16 mL, including liquid temporarily inside the pipette. These values illustrate conservation, not a culture recipe.

Hybridomas require survival selection and antibody-specificity screening, followed by cloning and repeated verification. A mean of one cell per well does not mean exactly one in every well. The lineage drawing assumes that single-cell origin has already been established.

At metaphase II, enucleation removes a spindle–chromosome complex rather than an ordinary interphase nucleus. Oocyte cytoplasm remains. The fusion shot starts after a donor cell has been placed in the perivitelline space; that placement procedure is not replayed in the shot.

<h2 id="try">Try it yourself</h2>

- Compare adhesion, suspension and contact limitation. A model response for one selected condition cannot describe every animal cell line.
- Pause passage to follow the same cell identities and liquid volumes. Horizontal pipette movement occurs above the vessel rims.
- Switch the selective-culture condition, then separately inspect target recognition. Change mean occupancy λ to compare empty, single-cell and multiple-cell wells.
- Track the removed MII complex, donor-cell contact, shared cytoplasm and early cleavage. Purple marks the donor nucleus; brown marks oocyte organelles.
- The observation journal is blank. Record actual material, conditions, observations and uncertainties; animation does not replace laboratory results or authentication.

<h2 id="deeper">Go deeper</h2>

**Culture model.** Logistic growth uses N(t)=K/[1+(K/N₀−1)e^(−rt)] with N₀=4,r=0.35 and illustrative capacities 32 or 72. Time and counts are teaching scales, not a fitted cell-line dataset. Added glyphs represent population readouts, not an individual mitotic lineage. Contact inhibition varies by cell line, and suspension cultures remain resource-limited.

The bicarbonate approximation is pH=6.1+log₁₀([HCO₃⁻]/(0.03×pCO₂)), with concentration in mmol/L and partial pressure in mmHg. At 760 mmHg total pressure and 24 mmol/L bicarbonate, 5% CO₂ gives about 7.42. Doubling CO₂ at fixed bicarbonate lowers pH by about 0.301. Other buffers and cellular metabolism are omitted, so this is not a universal medium specification.

**Hybridomas.** The chosen partner is an HGPRT-deficient, nonsecreting myeloma, a common modern teaching simplification rather than an exact reconstruction of the 1975 cell line. In this HAT system, that myeloma cannot persist and ordinary B cells have finite lifespans. Hybridomas with different specificities can still survive together. The binding shot illustrates specificity, not a complete quantitative assay with washing, background correction and controls.

Independent Poisson occupancy gives P(0)=e⁻λ,P(1)=λe⁻λ and P(≥2)=1−e⁻λ−λe⁻λ. Multiple occupancy is about 26.4% at λ=1 and 3.7% at λ=0.3. These percentages refer to all wells, not occupied wells. Clumping, dispensing error and growth differences can violate the assumptions. Clonality needs independent evidence.

**Nuclear transfer.** The somatic donor supplies the main nuclear genome, the oocyte usually supplies most mitochondria, and the recipient provides gestation. This does not rule out donor mitochondrial carryover. Reprogramming, epigenetics and developmental environment also matter. The animation preserves the combined two-dimensional circle area during fusion as a geometric constraint, not a measurement of real three-dimensional volume or membrane area. Cleavage partitions are a sectional schematic and do not predict developmental timing or live-birth success.
