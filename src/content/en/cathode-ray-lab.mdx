<h2 id="understand">Read charge-to-mass ratio from deflection</h2>

An electric field deflects cathode rays opposite the field direction. A magnetic field supplies another controlled force. When crossed fields give no deflection, v=E/B. Combining speed with magnetic curvature yields charge-to-mass ratio. Agreement across cathode materials and gases supported the universality of electrons.

Lenard’s metal-window experiment concerns penetration through matter. The textbook briefly mentions the result; this scene supplements its apparatus principle. It is distinct from alpha scattering and does not alone establish a nuclear model.

<h2 id="try">Switch the force acting on one beam</h2>

Compare electric-only, magnetic-only and balanced crossed fields. Change field strength and observe the screen. In balanced mode, E follows vB. The metal-window path varies relative foil thickness and transmitted signal.
