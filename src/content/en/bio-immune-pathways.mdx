<h2 id="understand">Identify the structure, then test the route</h2>

The 2015 mouse study identified lymphatic features in vessels along dural sinuses and tested their transport function. This reconstruction distinguishes lymphatic identity markers such as LYVE-1 and PROX1, an intravenous vascular label, and QDot655 introduced into cerebrospinal fluid. Their identities and injection compartments are different. The drawing is **not an original micrograph**. The lymphatic structure remains in the meninges, outside brain parenchyma.

Identity staining alone does not demonstrate functional transport. Following CSF tracer into vessels and toward deep cervical lymph nodes supplies a different kind of evidence. Conversely, observing movement alone cannot establish endothelial identity.

<h2 id="try">Keep recognition and helper conditions explicit</h2>

Turn off identity markers or CSF tracing and identify the missing evidence. Nearby vascular and lymphatic routes do not become the same vessel, and their labels are not interchangeable.

The adaptive sequence follows a selected T-dependent pathway. Presentation and helper-cell recognition precede B-cell activation, clonal expansion and plasma-cell output. Y-shaped antibody identities remain continuous and bind antigen **outside cells**. They do not enter infected cells to hunt intracellular pathogens.

The cytotoxic view instead follows contact with a cell presenting matching antigen and subsequent target-cell apoptosis. Additional processes must clear debris and released material. Target-cell death does not mean every pathogen instantly disappears.

Matching memory can accelerate a later response. A different antigen can still initiate a new primary response; it does not imply universal failure to respond.

<h2 id="deeper">Declared stages rather than clinical timing</h2>

The model orders presentation, help, activation, expansion and antibody output. An unmatched selected clone or absent helper condition cannot silently advance through the remaining stages. The chosen route does not represent every possible B- or T-cell activation mechanism.

Recall uses explicit teaching parameters: primary delay 0.4 and rate 4, versus delay 0.1 and rate 8 for a matching memory response. Time is normalized, not measured in days. Tests independently check the exponential response, return to primary behavior for a new antigen, absence of apoptosis before recognition, delayed antibody output and separation of structural from functional evidence.
