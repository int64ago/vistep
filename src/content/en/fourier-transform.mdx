## Calculate the discrete transform

<div className="formula">
  X[k] = Σ x[n] exp(−i2πkn/N)
  <br />
  x[n] = Σ X[k] exp(i2πkn/N) / N
</div>

There are N=64 samples at 64 Hz over one second, so bin spacing is 1 Hz. The implementation directly sums sine and cosine products rather than invoking an FFT or estimating a spectrum from a drawing. For a real record, positive-frequency amplitudes are 2|X[k]|/N except at DC and Nyquist, which use |X[k]|/N. Reconstruction preserves those exceptions.

The projection chapter gradually exposes the sine accumulation; the full DFT includes both sine and cosine directions. A rectangular observation window causes spectral leakage when a component does not complete an integer number of cycles. Leakage does not imply many new physical oscillators in the original signal. The model is noiseless and does not cover window design, aliasing or the continuous Fourier integral.
