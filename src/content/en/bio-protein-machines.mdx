<h2 id="understand">Matching counts do not establish matching function</h2>

Bovine insulin has two peptide chains: 21 residues in A and 30 in B. Count adjacent joins: 20 in A and 29 in B, for 49 peptide bonds. For open, unbranched peptide chains, the count is residues minus chains. The separate chain ends matter, and a different type of connection must not enter this count.

Track one oxygen and two hydrogens in the close-up. Relative to free amino acids, forming one peptide bond accounts for one water molecule in the net composition. No atoms disappear. This is a composition account, not the cellular reaction mechanism. Applying it to the two unmodified open chains gives a net difference of 49 water molecules; it does not mean that ribosomes or insulin processing actually release these waters.

Six cysteines form three disulfides: A6–A11 within A, and A7–B7 and A20–B19 between chains. The close-up shows sulfur atoms on side chains. These links add spatial constraints without joining the chain ends into one longer peptide or adding three peptide bonds.

<h2 id="try">Change connections, then check what follows</h2>

- Move through the atom account and back. The same oxygen and two hydrogens remain identifiable; the hydrogen retained on nitrogen is not counted twice.
- Exchange the different residues at A2 and A3. The counts remain 51 and 49 while the sequence changes. This is a candidate-sequence layout comparison, not a chemical mechanism for spontaneous residue swapping.
- Hiding disulfides changes visibility. Removing only disulfides changes the teaching graph while retaining two peptide chains and 49 peptide bonds. Neither operation calculates unfolding or activity.
- Rotate the same measured coordinates and inspect the sulfur atoms. Then switch to the new sequence candidate in the functional evidence view. A historical sample’s result cannot simply be copied to a new sample.

Textbook context: compulsory Biology 1, chapter 2, section 4, pp. 30–33. The p. 31 window closes the atom account, two-chain count and disulfide explanation; the p. 33 history window starts with the chains and closes with a separate functional comparison.

<h2 id="deeper">What does each evidence source establish?</h2>

<details>
<summary>The coordinate reference is not the historical synthetic sample</summary>

The reference is [RCSB PDB 2A3G](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2A3G), a bovine insulin T6 crystal structure published in 2005. The film selects 51 C-alpha positions in chains A/B and C-alpha, C-beta and S-gamma positions for six cysteines. Thick lines trace the coarse backbone, not every atomic covalent bond. The close-up S–S edges join actual sulfur positions. Rotation preserves three-dimensional distances; crossings in projection need not be chemical connections.

This is neither a structural record of the 1965 synthetic product nor a receptor-bound conformation. Layout changes and visibility controls do not simulate folding dynamics, energy minimization or activity. Fewer disulfide links mean fewer connectivity constraints; that alone does not specify a unique new conformation or prove zero activity.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the historical functional result come from?</summary>

Research participant Cheng-Lu Tsou recalled that purified, fully synthetic bovine insulin showed the same biological activity as natural insulin in a mouse convulsion bioassay in 1965. His account appeared in 1998 and was reprinted in _Chinese Bulletin of Life Sciences_ 27(6), pp. 778–779, section 2, in 2015. Read the [complete participant account](https://lifescience.sinh.ac.cn/webadmin/upload/20156777.pdf) or its [Chinese Academy of Sciences republication](https://cemcs.cas.cn/zt_197708/nydsucg60zn/wshm/202508/t20250826_7915418.html).

The view reconstructs the comparison between a natural reference and the purified synthetic sample. It supplies no invented doses, animal counts, response curves or original numerical values. The result marks mean “reported active,” not a live simulation or a modern statistical equivalence claim. Historical assay procedures are outside this lesson.

The [1966 total-synthesis paper’s bibliographic record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5960685/) verifies the original study’s identity, but its full original assay data were not available for this production. The functional view is explicitly a qualitative reconstruction supported by a participant account. Later structural coordinates or modern measurements on other samples are not substituted for the 1965 records.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why are disulfides not extra condensation waters?</summary>

The peptide account compares free amino acids with unmodified open peptide chains. Cells use activated substrates: ribosomal peptide-bond formation is not spontaneous condensation of two free amino acids as in this net-composition diagram.

Disulfide formation is redox chemistry between thiol groups. A complete molecular formula would separately account for six fewer hydrogens when six thiols form three disulfides; it would not add three condensation waters. This film does not calculate a complete molecular mass or give a disulfide-reduction procedure.

</details>
