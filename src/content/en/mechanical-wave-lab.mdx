<h2 id="understand">Track one object, then the overall shape</h2>

An upward or downward motion launches a pulse along a rope. The red marker remains on the same material segment, moving vertically rather than travelling with the pulse. With continuous driving, every segment repeats the source motion after a distance-dependent delay.

A human wave demonstrates delayed standing and crouching without people walking along the row. A soft spring is driven along its axis: coil spacing alternately compresses and expands while individual coils oscillate locally.
