<h2 id="understand">Three levels of evidence</h2>

Chicken oviduct cells, nucleated red blood cells and islet cells contain all three assayed genes but show different mRNA detection patterns. The table supports selective expression, not loss of the other genes. “Not detected” is not proof of absolute absence, and these three markers do not represent the complete transcriptome.

The two Linaria flower forms share the Lcyc sequence while differing in methylation and expression; the textbook also follows offspring flower types. Aᵛʸ/a mice show a yellow-to-dark coat range related to methylation and ectopic expression at an upstream element. A dark animal has not become a/a; dark pseudoagouti and the black recessive homozygote are distinct concepts.

The vestigial-fly experiment changes larval rearing conditions. Some adults developing at 31 °C have nearly normal-length wings, while their offspring reared at 25 °C retain vestigial wings. This supports an environmental influence on trait expression without establishing a particular enzyme mechanism or suggesting that adult wings instantly change with temperature.

<h2 id="deeper">What the drawings cannot establish</h2>

<details>
<summary>Limits of the illustrations and inference</summary>

Cell structures are simplified. The detection matrix preserves the textbook’s plus/minus observations, without invented gel bands or molecular concentrations. Oval, Glob and Ins are display abbreviations, not precise database identifiers.

Flower illustrations emphasize bilateral versus radial organization and spur number rather than reconstructing whole plants to scale. The two F₂ icons denote types, not sample counts or an exact segregation ratio. CpG letters and sparse methyl marks are schematic, not actual Lcyc or Aᵛʸ sequence maps or estimates of genome-wide methylation. Tissue states may be mosaic; maintenance and resetting depend on context. Not every acquired trait is inherited across generations.

Mouse coats illustrate the Aᵛʸ/a phenotype range without dietary advice or disease prediction. The fly display uses only the textbook’s 25 °C, 31 °C and offspring 25 °C conditions. It supplies no continuous temperature curve, effect size or frequency. Wing lengths are categorical drawings without invented millimeter scales. Enzyme-based developmental explanations remain hypotheses to test.
