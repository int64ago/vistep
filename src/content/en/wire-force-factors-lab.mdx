<h2 id="understand">Count the current-carrying length inside the field</h2>

Three identical horseshoe magnets sit side by side, approximating one uniform field at the perpendicular suspended wire. Keep the contacts and magnets fixed while increasing current: the equilibrium deflection grows. Keep current fixed and change contacts from 2–3 to 1–4: more wire contributes to the force.

The effective length ℓ is the intersection of the current-carrying interval and the magnetic region. Total wire length, contact spacing, and immersed current-carrying length may differ. The force magnitude is F = B|I|ℓ; signed readings distinguish opposite force directions.

<h2 id="try">Change one factor at a time</h2>

Compare contact pairs, then shift the wire partly outside the field. The contact spacing stays unchanged while effective length decreases. Set current to zero, then reverse it. Finally change field strength while holding current and contacts fixed.

This follows PEP Required Book 3, printed pages109–110. Moving the wire partly outside the field is an added comparison. The field-strength control represents another field strength, not an assertion that adding identical magnets side by side increases B at one point.
