<h2 id="understand">Scattering provides different kinds of evidence</h2>

Alpha-particle angular scattering reveals concentrated positive charge and mass. Compton wavelength shifts test photon energy and momentum. These are distinct observations and conclusions.

The foil view enlarges selected single-nucleus encounters. Most particles pass nearly straight; rare large deflections matter. Four chosen paths do not represent measured probabilities. The Compton view retains an incident reference while calculating the shifted peak and electron recoil direction together.

<h2 id="try">Compare controlled variables</h2>

Select alpha trajectories with different impact parameters. Then switch to Compton scattering and vary the detector angle from nearly forward to backward, checking the wavelength-shift limits.
