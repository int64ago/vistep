<h2 id="understand">Determine weight before weighing charge</h2>

The textbooks mention Millikan’s measurement without giving a complete classroom procedure. This scene supplements that historical context with the measurement principle.

The no-field terminal speed estimates drop radius. The balancing voltage then determines charge. A negatively charged drop experiences an upward force in a downward field.

<h2 id="try">Compare charge with balancing voltage</h2>

Keep drop size fixed, change the electron count and adjust voltage until speed approaches zero. More charge needs less balancing voltage. The display gives charge magnitude; the actual charge is negative. Downward velocity is positive.
