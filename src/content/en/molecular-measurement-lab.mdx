<h2 id="understand">Connect visible evidence to invisible molecules</h2>

The gold ring marks a carbon tracer. A microscope records its changing direction but does not resolve the individual water molecules striking it. A separate impact sketch makes the explanation visible: impacts arrive from every side, yet their combined effect is briefly unbalanced and keeps changing direction. Both views share seeded increments to connect their directions. The sketch is neither a microscope photograph nor drawn to scale. Brownian motion is **indirect evidence** of molecular thermal motion.

The temperature comparison keeps particle radius and random inputs fixed. The gold and teal records differ because diffusion changes: warming increases thermal motion, and lower water viscosity further increases diffusion. Thirty-second position links are sampling records, not the true intervening path.

Two cylinders begin with fifty millilitres each of water and ethanol. Adding the readings predicts one hundred, yet a representative settled reading is about ninety-six. Follow the structure sketch: water remains water, ethanol remains ethanol, and every illustrated molecule persists. Neighbours, interactions and liquid structure change. **Mass conservation does not require additive volumes.** A liquid is not a collection of rigid balls filling fixed gaps. The sketch explains that distinction; it does not calculate the four-millilitre contraction.

The oil film takes the reasoning from motion and volume to length. First find the pure oleic-acid volume in one diluted drop, then measure the stable film area. Dividing volume by area gives a molecular-scale thickness only under the uniform-monolayer assumption.

<h2 id="try">Predict before changing a control</h2>

- Hold temperature fixed and halve the effective tracer radius. Diffusion doubles; root-mean-square displacement at the same elapsed time increases by √2. Individual steps do not all double.
- Replay the mixture and check that both illustrated molecular counts persist. Does lower volume imply missing mass? Without leakage or evaporation, it does not.
- One millilitre of solution, fifty drops, 500-fold dilution and 200 square centimetres give about two nanometres. Raising the area reading to 220 square centimetres lowers the estimate to about 1.82 nanometres.
- If the film overlaps into multiple layers, more precise readings still cannot identify average film thickness with one molecule's scale.

It omits convection, particle interactions and focus-depth changes, while including temperature-dependent water viscosity. The scale bar shows field magnification as parameters change. The selected tracer stays in view; other particles may leave it. Four groups of impact arrows represent aggregate imbalance, not four actual molecular trajectories, impact counts or measured forces.

The twelve water and four ethanol glyphs are a trackable sample, not a composition ratio. Carbon-bound hydrogens are omitted from ethanol. Dashed links indicate neighbouring interactions, not newly formed chemical bonds. About 96 mL is a near-room-temperature representative result; precise contraction depends on temperature, composition and experimental conditions. It requires measurements or a state model. Hydrogen-bond networks and local liquid structure are more complex than a fixed-gap analogy.
