<h2 id="understand">From a shared law to an experimental reading</h2>

The Moon–Earth test compares independent acceleration estimates; a torsion balance converts laboratory attraction into a measurable angle; the cannon thought experiment connects falling with orbital motion.

<h2 id="try">Swap masses and change launch speed</h2>

Change attractor mass or position and compare responses released from rest. Vary the cannon’s speed continuously. Paths stop on the surface rather than pass through Earth.
