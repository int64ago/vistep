<h2 id="understand">Separate experimental evidence from mechanism</h2>

Reading triplets from a fixed start makes a one-base insertion or deletion capable of changing many downstream codons. A net change of 3 bases preserves the downstream frame while potentially altering the local peptide. Particular mutant combinations in Crick’s work partially restored function; arbitrary three-base changes are not necessarily harmless. The displayed sequence is illustrative, not the original T4 gene sequence.

Poly-U links UUU to phenylalanine. The textbook’s single-amino-acid tubes simplify a historical study that also used labeled mixtures and other designs. The second display reproduces one control series from original Table 6: ¹⁴C-Phe incorporation in counts per minute per milligram protein, not product concentration or a count of visible chains.

<h2 id="try">Follow conditions and information</h2>

- Insert or delete bases and follow the original letters into their new triplets.
- Remove poly-U or ribosomes from the qualitative textbook reconstruction.
- Pause transcription and compare the template’s 3′ → 5′ direction with RNA growth at 5′ → 3′.
- Follow the peptide from the P-site tRNA to the A-site tRNA, then through translocation. A release factor recognizes stop.

<h2 id="deeper">What has been simplified?</h2>

<summary>Short sequences, directions and exceptions</summary>

The example AUG–UUU–GGC–AAA–UAA gives Met–Phe–Gly–Lys. Anticodons pair antiparallel to mRNA, amino acids attach at tRNA 3′ ends, and peptide extension proceeds from N to C. The ribosome contains rRNA and proteins; its drawn outline shows functions rather than atomic geometry or scale. Letter, peptide and tRNA identities persist, with translocation derived from the same codon spacing.

The film omits eukaryotic RNA processing, nuclear export, complete initiation factors, energy bookkeeping, wobble pairing, ribosome disassembly and protein folding. The exit tunnel and tRNA acceptor geometry are unfolded into the drawing plane to keep the chain visible. Length and time are compressed.

The default uses the standard code: 20 amino acids and stop signals. Selenocysteine insertion at UGA requires special context and machinery, beyond this model. Alternative bacterial initiation does not make every internal GUG a methionine codon. Special cell-free poly-U initiation is kept separate from the ordinary AUG mechanism.
