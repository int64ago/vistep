<h2 id="understand">Understand the principle</h2>

Two learning positions are kept distinct: organic synthesis and retrosynthesis on PEP Selective Compulsory 3 pages 84–90 are structural/process demonstrations; laboratory activity 2 on pages 99–100 concerns functional-group tests. Planning does not imply a synthesis was performed.

Diethyl oxalate suggests oxalic acid and two ethanols. Six carbon identities are retained; OH/H added during planning complete prospective precursors, not atoms appearing in a closed decomposition. Forward esterification eliminates two waters. One ethene branch supplies ethanol by hydration; the other passes through 1,2-dichloroethane and ethylene glycol to oxalic acid. Conditions, selectivity, isolation and alternative routes require further evaluation.

The four tests concern 1-hexene with bromine/acidified permanganate, hydrolyzed 1-bromobutane followed by aqueous sampling and acidification, phenol bromination/ferric color, and ethanal reduction of fresh Cu(OH)₂. Use independent portions. Permanganate oxidation is not unique to double bonds; aldehydes also consume bromine water, while benzene can merely extract it.

<h2 id="try">Try it yourself</h2>

In the first finite set, ethanol mixes with water and CCl₄ forms the lower organic layer. The upper-layer pair is distinguished by bromine consumption versus extraction. In the second set, ferric color, copper reduction and correctly pretreated chloride tests isolate phenol, propanal and 2-chloropropane, leaving 1-propanol. Adding observations actually filters candidates. Invalid halide preparation cannot be added as valid evidence.

These are conditional responses for named candidates, not a universal procedure for arbitrary mixtures and concentrations. Permanganate oxidation of alcohols requires appropriate conditions and is not promised instantaneous. The incomplete-preparation counterexample specifically concerns residual alkaline interference.

One aspirin aliquot tests acidic behavior; another undergoes acid hydrolysis to salicylic acid and acetic acid. Bicarbonate neutralizes excess sulfuric acid before a ferric test under suitable conditions. Salicylic-acid impurity in the original tablet may also test positive, so real samples need original, hydrolyzed and blank comparisons. The invalid-medium control withholds interpretation rather than predicting no color at every acidity.

<h2 id="deeper">Go deeper</h2>

The branch-yield extension allocates 1 ethene equivalent to the oxalic-acid branch and 2 to the ethanol branch, with identical per-step yield y and no recycle. Available oxalic acid is y³; ethanol supports y. Final esterification gives min(y³,y)×y = y⁴. At y = 0.7 this is 24.01%, a hypothetical calculation rather than textbook or industrial measurement.

The NMR plots show environments and group areas, not acquisition, splitting or fitted peaks. Ideal ratios are 3:2:1 for ethanol, 3:1 for acetic acid and 3:2:3 for ethyl acetate. This scene uses its own numbering. On textbook page 100, figure 1 is ethyl acetate, figure 2 ethanol and figure 3 acetic acid. Exchangeable-proton shifts and integrals depend on sample conditions.
